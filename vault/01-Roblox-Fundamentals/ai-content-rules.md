# AI content rules

## Assets
- AI-generated clothing, models, and code are allowed. No disclosure is required for AI-made assets.
- They must follow Community Standards, Marketplace Policy, and IP rules. The uploader is responsible, not the tool. "The AI wrote it" is not a defence.
- All inputs and outputs go through moderation (text filter, image review, asset review).

## AI interactions (different rules)
- Games where player interaction triggers generative responses must disclose it in the Content Maturity questionnaire.
- Extended chatbot-like interaction (continuous conversation, memory across sessions): secondary sources say it requires a **Restricted (18+)** label and a visible notice such as "This is an AI-powered conversation, not human. It may make mistakes." UNVERIFIED: the primary content-maturity page defines AI interactions in its own section (checked 2026-11-09) but this note has not confirmed the 18+ requirement or the notice text there.
- AI output must match the game's maturity level.

## How Roblox treats AI slop
Roblox does not filter AI content or employ curators for it. From Q2 2026 it optimised Recommended for You for long-term retention. Reported result: by end of Q3 2026 about 40% of the weekly top 100 by impressions were made in the previous three months (UNVERIFIED). Translation: new games can break in fast; generic games get buried fast. This is why the studio's anti-slop gate exists ([[17-Development-Templates/anti-slop-checklist]]).
