# Video tutorials watched

## Do this
- To watch another video during a job, run `brewix watch <link>`. It saves the captions as a timestamped transcript and up to 100 frames into `runs/videos/<id>/`; read `watch.md` first, then the frames it lists.
- Prefer the official docs when a video and the docs disagree; videos lag behind Studio updates.
- The lessons from these videos are in [[01-Roblox-Fundamentals/studio-ui-and-shortcuts]], [[10-Art-Environment/map-building-from-tutorials]], [[10-Art-Environment/lighting-recipes-from-tutorials]] and [[01-Roblox-Fundamentals/publishing-steps]].
