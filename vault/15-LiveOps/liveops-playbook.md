# Live ops playbook (zero spend)

## Rhythm
- Small update every week or two beats one big update a quarter: players and the algorithm both reward regular change.
- Every update has one named goal (fix bounce, raise D1, raise D7) from [[06-Retention/decision-tree]].

## Free events
- Limited-time quest or challenge, double rewards weekend, holiday decoration swap, community goal.
- Announce in the game itself and in the update name; no paid ads.

## Order of work
1. Fix what breaks the first session. 2. Fix what stops day-2 returns. 3. Add depth for regulars. 4. Then monetization polish.
