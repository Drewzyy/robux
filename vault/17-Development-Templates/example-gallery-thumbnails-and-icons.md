# Example gallery: thumbnails and icons
Original diagrams drawn for this vault so agents and the owner can see what the rules look like. Nothing here is copied from a real game.

## Do this
- Icon is a separate 512x512 crop, readable at about 150x150: one face or object, 2 to 3 saturated colours.
- Thumbnail is 1920x1080, under 3 MB; avatar 30 to 40% of the frame, eyes visible, facing camera.
- Maximum 3 elements: one avatar, one prize or hazard, one text of 1 to 3 words.
- Text 20 to 30% of frame height, thick outline, upper or lower third.
- Show a moment that exists in the game; the first 10 seconds must keep the promise.
- Use your own characters and art. No catalog items, logos or lookalikes.

## Good icon
![[attachments/examples/icon-good.svg]]
*One shocked avatar, one gem, two-word title on a strip.*

What it teaches:
- Reads at 150 px: the face is the focal point.
- Complementary blue and orange, high saturation.
- Title is short and outlined.

## Bad icon
![[attachments/examples/icon-bad.svg]]
*Crowded scene, tiny figures, long muddy text.*

What it teaches:
- Nothing to look at, nothing to read at small size.
- Title is a sentence; it disappears on a phone.
- Low contrast colours blend together.

## Good thumbnail
![[attachments/examples/thumbnail-good.svg]]
*Large avatar, one cyan prize, two-word text.*

What it teaches:
- Three elements: avatar, prize, text.
- Arrow-free story: shock plus prize raises a question.
- Colours from the game, pushed to full saturation.

## Bad thumbnail
![[attachments/examples/thumbnail-bad.svg]]
*Ten small figures, paragraph text, no focus.*

What it teaches:
- More than three elements and no story.
- Text under 4 words rule broken twice.
- Shows process, not one moment or prize.

## How to use these pictures
- Open the note, read the badges on each picture, then compare your own screenshot side by side.
- Keep the SVG files as the reference; redraw in Figma or Studio with your own art.
- If a picture and a rule note disagree, the rule note wins; fix the picture.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy: the principle shown (layout zones, timings, counts, order of steps).
- Never copy: names, art, logos, characters or music from real games. Every picture here uses our own shapes.
- Zero spend: all of this is built with free tools; no paid assets or ads.
- Children play: no pressure timers, no shame copy, odds always shown, purchases only from a clear player tap.
