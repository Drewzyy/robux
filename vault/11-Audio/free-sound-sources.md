# Free sound sources

## Do this
- First choice for UI and game sounds: **Kenney** audio packs (Interface Sounds, UI Audio, Impact Sounds, RPG Audio). CC0: free for anything, no credit needed.
- Big professional library: **Sonniss GDC bundles**, royalty-free for commercial use, no attribution (not for AI training).
- **Freesound**: filter to CC0 only; CC-BY needs credit and CC-BY-NC is not allowed in a game that earns Robux.
- Custom one-off sounds: the ForgeGUI sheet ([[11-Audio/forgegui]]).
- Upload sounds to Roblox yourself; agents only list what is needed in sounds.json.

## Not usable for free
- **ElevenLabs** sound effects: the free plan does not allow commercial use, and a game that earns Robux is commercial. It needs a paid plan, which breaks zero spend.
- BBC Sound Effects: non-commercial only.
