## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: fast first win, a visible next goal, friends, and a reason to return tomorrow. Never copy another game's names, art or exact reward values.
- No dark patterns: no fake timers, guilt messages or streak punishment aimed at children.
- Every retention change needs a named metric and a before and after check.
