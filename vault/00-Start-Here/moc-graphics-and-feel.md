## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: readable silhouettes, one accent colour, mood from lighting. Never copy another game's art, logos, icons or music.
- Build from primitives and your own meshes; no Toolbox, no paid assets.
- Check performance budgets before adding more detail.
