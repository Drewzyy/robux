## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: sell convenience, style and boosts; keep the free path fun. Never copy another game's product names, icons or exact prices.
- Purchase prompts only from a player tap; odds always shown for random items.
- Money numbers change: check the dated source in each note before relying on them.
