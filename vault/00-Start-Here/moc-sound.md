## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: short, pitch-varied feedback sounds on every action and a settings menu with sliders. Never copy another game's music, voices or sound effects.
- Zero spend: free licensed or self-generated audio only, and record the license.
- Keep volumes safe for children and never autoplay loud sounds.
