# Age groups and what they want

Roblox spans young children to adults. Design for a primary band and make the rest welcome. These are tendencies, not rules. See [[03-Game-Psychology/player-psychology]].

## Do this
- Choose one primary age band before design; write it in the game design doc.
- Under 13: use short words, icons over text, big buttons, clear goals, quick rewards, and low-risk loss.
- 13-17: add social status, customization, ranks, clips, and skill expression; keep onboarding under 60 s.
- 17+: offer depth, optimization, build-crafting, and longer sessions; avoid childish tone but stay safe.
- Keep text at roughly grade 3-5 reading level for mass appeal; one sentence per hint.
- Never design manipulative spend prompts for kids: no countdown pressure on purchases, no fake scarcity. See [[03-Game-Psychology/loss-aversion-used-fairly]].
- Use Roblox's maturity and age guidance tools for content ratings; check the current rules in the Creator Hub.
- Test with at least two real players from your target band, with a guardian's permission when minors.

## Under 13
- Wants: fun immediately, collecting, pets, friends, being noticed, simple wins.
- Design moves: obvious next step, bright feedback, cute characters, shared play with a sibling or friend. See [[03-Game-Psychology/play-with-friends]].
- Risks: frustration quits, overspending, unsafe chat. Use filtered chat, parental-friendly pricing, and no pressure.
- Session shape: 10-25 min, frequent breaks.

## 13-17
- Wants: status, mastery, belonging, identity, being first, trying what is new.
- Design moves: leaderboards, ranked ladders, cosmetics that signal skill, clip tools. See [[03-Game-Psychology/social-status-and-flex]].
- Risks: toxicity, pay-to-win resentment, grind fatigue.
- Session shape: 20-60 min, evening peaks, with friends.

## 17+
- Wants: depth, systems, challenge, optimization, story, fair economies.
- Design moves: build choices, advanced stats, strong long-term goals, competitive modes, mature but policy-compliant themes.
- Risks: low tolerance for filler, strong dislike of paywalls.
- Session shape: 30-90 min, often scheduled.

## How to build it in Roblox
- Add a settings page: text size, reduced motion, chat off, color-blind options.
- Layer complexity: simple default UI, an advanced tab that opens at level 10 or on request.
- Use Roblox's built-in age and chat features; do not collect age yourself.
- Check analytics by platform (phone, PC, console) since age and device correlate (UNVERIFIED as a general rule).
- Make tutorial skippable for experienced players.

## Numbers (starting points)
- Under 13: first reward under 20 s, hint text under 10 words.
- 13-17: first status item visible within 5 min.
- 17+: first meaningful build choice within 10 min.

## Mistakes
- Designing for adults and marketing to kids, or the reverse.
- Complex menus for young players.
- Violence or themes outside the age rating.
- Overusing slang that dates quickly.

## For our Roblox games
Copy the principle: pick a band, fit reading level, pacing, and social style to it. Never copy trademarked characters or manipulative tactics, and never gather personal data about players. Follow current Roblox policy at all times.
