# Lessons from classic games

| Game | What kept players | Borrow it as |
|---|---|---|
| Club Penguin | a shared town with hangout spots, monthly parties that came and went, a home to decorate | a hub with places to hang out; a monthly themed event; personal space |
| Animal Crossing | real-time days, small daily tasks, decorating, visiting friends | things that change daily; decorate-and-show; friend visits |
| RuneScape | many skills to level, long goals, visible levels | several parallel progress bars; titles that show mastery |
| Minecraft | building anything, playing together, no wrong way | a creative mode or plot; co-op building |
| Pokemon | collecting, completing a set, trading | collection book with silhouettes for missing items; trading |
| Adopt Me | free starter pet, populated servers, trading, weekly content | free first item in minute one; trading; steady small updates |
| Tamagotchi | something that needs you | a pet or garden that grows while away (never dies or loses progress) |

## Do this
- Pick two or three, not all. Name which ones in the design doc.
- The common thread: a place to be with friends, something to show off, and something new on a schedule.
