# What players love and hate, across niches
Ranked from the 15 niche review notes. Frequency means how many of the 15 niches list the theme; it shows how universal a rule is, not how big the effect is.

## Do this
- Ship every game with the top 5 loves built in: fast reward, friend play, fair challenge, a visible next goal, a satisfying feel.
- Run the top 5 hates as a blocker list before launch: pay to win, bugs, lag, grind walls, hidden odds.
- Monetize with cosmetics, time savers and rewarded choices only; show odds and prices where the player taps.
- Test the first 10 minutes on a low-end phone with 150 ms ping every build.
- Never open a purchase prompt on its own; only from a player tap (audience includes children).
- After launch, replace our tally with real data: Roblox analytics leave rates and player comments.

## Top 15 loves
1. Fast, visible reward and progress | appears in 11 of 15 | e.g. simulator, tycoon, rng, mobile, tower-defense, survival
2. Playing with friends | appears in 11 of 15 | e.g. roleplay, horror, minigame, survival, shooter, racing
3. Fair challenge where failure feels like my fault | appears in 9 of 15 | e.g. obby, fighting, shooter, tower-defense, racing
4. Collecting and showing off rares or cosmetics | appears in 9 of 15 | e.g. rng, simulator, dress-up, shooter, roleplay
5. Freedom and creativity | appears in 7 of 15 | e.g. roleplay, dress-up, tycoon, survival
6. Satisfying feel (hit, jump, handling, cascades) | appears in 8 of 15 | e.g. fighting, obby, racing, shooter, match3
7. Atmosphere and mood | appears in 4 of 15 | e.g. horror, story, survival
8. Short sessions and instant restart | appears in 8 of 15 | e.g. obby, minigame, mobile, racing
9. Variety and fresh updates | appears in 9 of 15 | e.g. simulator, minigame, obby, dress-up, roleplay
10. A clear next goal on screen | appears in 8 of 15 | e.g. tycoon, simulator, tower-defense, survival
11. Free retries or no-lives play | appears in 3 of 15 | e.g. mobile, obby, racing
12. Rewarded ads or boosts that the player chooses | appears in 2 of 15 | e.g. mobile, simulator
13. Meaningful choices | appears in 3 of 15 | e.g. story, tower-defense, survival
14. Co-op story or squad runs | appears in 4 of 15 | e.g. horror, survival, story, tower-defense
15. Cheap or free ways to look good | appears in 4 of 15 | e.g. dress-up, roleplay, shooter, racing

## Top 15 hates
1. Pay to win (stats, summons, cars, guns sold for Robux) | appears in 11 of 15 | fixes: cosmetic or time-saver only, free route to everything
2. Bugs, crashes, soft locks | appears in 13 of 15 | fixes: daily first-10-minute test, escape button, autosave
3. Lag and weak performance on phones | appears in 12 of 15 | fixes: part and entity caps, low-end test
4. Grind walls with no end | appears in 8 of 15 | fixes: rebirth or reset, skip earned by play, pity timers
5. Hidden or bad odds, gambling feel | appears in 5 of 15 | fixes: show odds, pity counter, free rolls by play
6. Cheaters and exploiters | appears in 5 of 15 | fixes: server validation, rate limits, report button
7. Unfair deaths or unfair matchmaking | appears in 8 of 15 | fixes: skill matching, readable hazards, spawn protection
8. Boring repetitive loop | appears in 9 of 15 | fixes: change the loop every 10-15 minutes
9. Too short or thin for the promise | appears in 5 of 15 | fixes: replay hooks, honest store page
10. Ads and popups (forced, fake close buttons) | appears in 3 of 15, but top in mobile | fixes: rewarded only, one-time remove-ads, player-tap prompts
11. Toxic players, griefing, creepy behavior | appears in 6 of 15 | fixes: mute, report, block, safe chat, base protection
12. Confusing or long forced tutorial | appears in 6 of 15 | fixes: 60 second guided first goal, no text walls
13. Difficulty spikes that push spending | appears in 4 of 15 | fixes: fail-rate per level dashboard, smooth curve
14. Scams and trade theft | appears in 3 of 15 | fixes: confirm screen, 5 second lock, scam report
15. Updates that nerf or wipe progress | appears in 5 of 15 | fixes: compensation, public patch notes, beta server

## Monetization complaints and fixes
- Pay to win: the biggest single mobile complaint group, 34 percent of 1-3 star reviews in one vendor analysis (UNVERIFIED). Fix: sell speed, cosmetics and convenience; keep a free path to every power.
- Popups and ads: players accept ads they choose and hate forced ones and fake close buttons (AppFollow, vendor, UNVERIFIED). Fix: rewarded only, a one-time remove-ads pass, no popups until a player taps.
- Prices: complaints about overpriced items and cash-grab feel recur in forum lists. Fix: show price in Robux next to value, offer cheap starter items, no escalating roll prices.
- Scams: trade theft and fake 'free Robux' offers. Fix: trade confirm with both sides shown and a 5 second lock, report button, warning text.
- Gambling feel: press calls random rewards for children gambling. Fix: percent odds on the button, pity counter, no cash-out.
- Urgency and FOMO: limited-time passes draw manipulation complaints. Fix: bring limited items back and avoid countdown pressure.

## How to use the ranking
- Score a design: give one point for each of the top 15 loves present and subtract one for each of the 15 hates present. Aim for 10 or more.
- Break ties by cost to the player: bugs and lag block play, so fix them before polishing loves.
- Re-run the score after each playtest with 5 players who have not seen the game.

## Caveats
- Evidence is mostly vendor blogs, forum threads and press; counts here are tallies, not measured.
- Children and adults complain about different things; adults dominate Steam and app reviews, so Roblox's younger players may weigh friends and cosmetics more (UNVERIFIED).
- Frequency of a hate does not equal churn impact; validate with our analytics.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principles: reward fast, play together, stay fair, stay stable, be honest about odds and prices.
- Never copy names, characters, art, music or logos from any game named in the sources, and never use dark patterns on a young audience.
