# Streaks and daily rewards

## Do this
- Evidence is mixed: login bonuses can lift day-2 returns without lifting total playtime. Treat them as a nudge, not a strategy. Fix the core loop first.
- Tie the reward to playing, not to logging in: "play one round today" or "finish today's quest", not "press claim".
- Make rewards useful in play (a new ability, a cosmetic to show friends), not just small currency.
- Escalate over 7 days with a highlight on day 7, then repeat with variety.
- Forgive: one free streak freeze per week, or let players repair a broken streak with in-game currency they earned (not Robux). Losing a 20-day streak to a sick day makes kids quit.
- Show the streak where friends can see it (overhead tag or leaderboard).
- Never punish missing days by taking things away.

## Better appointments than login bonuses
Things that grow while away (gardens, eggs, crafting timers), weekly community events, and "come back at 7 pm for the boss" world events.
