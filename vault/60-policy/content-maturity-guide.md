# Content maturity guide

Every experience completes the Content Maturity questionnaire in Creator Dashboard.

| Label | May contain (summary) |
|---|---|
| Minimal | "May contain occasional mild violence and/or light unrealistic blood." |
| Mild | "May contain repeated mild violence, heavy unrealistic blood, mild fear-based content, and/or mild crude humor." |
| Moderate | moderate violence, light realistic blood, moderate fear, moderate crude humour, unplayable gambling content |
| Restricted (age-verified users 18+ only) | "May contain strong violence, heavy realistic blood, moderate crude humor, romantic themes, unplayable gambling content, the presence of alcohol, and/or strong language." |

Separate sections of the same page (not part of the Restricted label text): social hangouts with private spaces are only available to age-verified players 18+; AI interactions are games where "a user can interact with a generative AI model in any way that triggers a response from the model", with criteria such as cross-session memory for extended interactions. Read those sections directly when a game has either.

Correction (2026-11-09): the research report listed social hangouts, private spaces, extended AI interactions, and fear inside the Restricted label. The primary page does not.

## Rules
- No questionnaire: Roblox restricts playability for all players.
- After an update that changes answers, update and resubmit.
- Inaccurate answers can lead to moderation of the game and the account.
- Maturity does not cover what players bring (avatar clothing).

Studio default: aim for Minimal or Mild. The audience is mostly minors (about 35-36% under 13 and 38% aged 13-17 among age-checked users, per secondary reporting of Roblox's 2026 figures).
