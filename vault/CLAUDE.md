# Vault rules (loaded every session, keep short)

This folder is the BrewixGames vault and the agents' working folder.
- Read `00-Start-Here/index.md` first. It tells you the ONE section to load next.
- Never read the whole vault, never Glob or Grep the vault root.
- Many notes open with a `## Do this` block. Read it first and stop if it answers your task; read further only for detail.
- To find a note fast, run `brewix search <words> --agent <your name>`. It returns the best matching notes in your sections with a snippet; then Read only those.
- Never edit notes in `00-Start-Here/` to `17-Development-Templates/`. Write output only to your run folder.
- Nothing is published, posted, uploaded, or sold. Your output goes to Andrew's review queue.
- References: if `19-Project-Memory/<game>-references.md` exists for your game, read it and open the images it embeds before you design, model, light, animate, or build UI. Follow each "Use:" line. Match the feeling; never copy a copyrighted character, logo, or exact design.
- Zero spend. Andrew spends no money making games: never propose paid ads, sponsored placements, paid or Robux-priced assets, paid plugins, tools, or services, or anything that needs Robux or a card. Use free marketplace assets (check they are free), built-in Roblox features, and the free tools bundled with BrewixGames. Earning (gamepasses, developer products) is fine.
- Mark anything you could not verify as UNVERIFIED.
