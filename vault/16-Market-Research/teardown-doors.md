# Teardown: Doors

A cooperative horror game: up to 4 or more players move door by door through a haunted hotel, avoiding entities that appear without warning. By LSPLASH, who also run dozens of other experiences.

## Do this
- Make a room the unit of play. Each door is a fresh puzzle, with a short guaranteed safe moment, then a possible scare.
- Use a small number of named entities, each with a rule players learn (hide, look away, run). A learnable rule turns fear into skill and gives players something to teach friends.
- Count the run in doors. A visible door number gives an instant goal ("beat my 50").
- Support 1 to 4 players with scaling. Solo should be playable and co-op should be easier and funnier.
- Ship content as a new floor or area, not just a patch. One large update every few months carries a long tail (Hotel+ in Jan 2023, a Mines floor reported in 2024).
- Let death be a lesson. Show what killed the player and give a revive path that costs time, not only Robux.
- Sell revive and cosmetic items, not progress that blocks the free path (check [[07-Monetization/paid-random-items-checklist]] for any crate).

## 1. Identity
- Developer: LSPLASH. The game passed 3B visits per Pocket Tactics (date unread). Hotel+ released 2023-01-29 per one summary.
- CCU: one snapshot showed about 20.8K for a rush-mode variant, up 29% in 24 hours. The main game's all-time peak is UNVERIFIED. A rolearn snippet says 14.1M but it is unclear what that counts.
- Update cadence: major floor releases plus smaller event drops. Exact days UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Core loop
Open the next door, solve the room, survive entities, collect items, reach the next numbered room. Verb every 5 s: move, look, hide, pick up. Reward every 30 s: a new door number or an item.

## 3. Progression
Knobs or currency, items, badges, floors, an endless mode and shop upgrades. Details UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Monetization
Revives, items, boosts and cosmetics. Full list UNVERIFIED. No evidence found of aggressive pay-to-win.

## 5. First 60 seconds (INFERRED)
Elevator spawn, ordinary corridor, first door, a gentle first scare before 60 s. The tension is the hook.

## 6. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: scary clips on short video, a friend asks you to play.
- Stay: the next door, a new entity, the urge to beat your friend's room number.
- Return: new floors, events, entity lore for theory-crafters, a community that makes content.

## 7. Social
Small co-op groups, a content-creator ecosystem (many "Doors" fan games). Co-op is why it spreads: horror is better with friends.

## 8. Packaging and performance
Dark icon with an eye and doors. Corridor rooms are cheap to render, which helps mobile (INFERRED).

## Numbers
- 3B visits (Pocket Tactics); 20.8K on one mode in a 2026 snapshot (RoWatcher).

## Mistakes to avoid
- Jumpscares with no rule. Players quit if they cannot learn from death.
- Forcing 4-player lobbies. Solo and duo must work.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: room-by-room progress, learnable monster rules, co-op scaling, floor-sized updates.
- Never copy: the Doors entity names and designs (Rush, Seek, Figure and similar), hotel layout, music, name or UI. Create original monsters and a different setting.
