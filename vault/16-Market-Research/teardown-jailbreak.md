# Teardown: Jailbreak

Badimo's cops-and-robbers open world. Players start in prison. Prisoners escape and rob locations; police stop them. A top-3 Roblox game for years, now far below its prime.

## Do this
- Give two teams opposed goals in one shared map, so every player makes a story for another.
- Make the heist the unit of content: each robbery site has a start, an objective, a payout and a time. Add a new site, not a new currency.
- Let vehicles and gear be the visible status layer. A rare car on the street advertises the player.
- Revamp the map as a content event. The June 13, 2026 revamp (new canyons, a new city, a new UI) coincided with the all-time 173K peak.
- Keep new-player routes safe in the first 5 minutes (protected spawn, an easy first robbery).
- Update the game on a published schedule. A calendar page for events is something players look up (a Jailbreak events schedule page exists).
- Retain old players with something new at least every month. Revenue and CCU both slid when the cadence slowed.

## 1. Identity
- Developer: Badimo. About 8B visits (Profitable). All-time peak 173K, June 2026 (RoWatcher snippet). Typical: 5K 7-day average, 6K to 11K live in mid-2026, down 31% over 30 days at the time of that snapshot.
- Update cadence: standalone content updates, no seasonal structure found (search summary).

## 2. Core loop
Escape prison or arrest criminals, rob sites or patrol, earn cash, buy vehicles and gear, ride, repeat. Verb every 5 s: drive or run. Reward every 30 s: a robbery stage or a payout.

## 3. Progression
Cash, vehicles, weapons, ranks, battle pass or season rewards (UNVERIFIED), vehicle skins. Collections matter.

## 4. Monetization
Gamepasses and developer products for vehicles, cash and extras. Prices UNVERIFIED. A revenue model estimate exists but is not a fact.

## 5. First 60 seconds (INFERRED)
Spawn in prison, a goal is obvious: escape. The first robbery is a few minutes away. Better onboarding is a known weak spot for older open-world games (general).

## 6. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: brand recognition, friends, vehicles.
- Stay: a large map with many activities, the thrill of chases.
- Return: map revamps, seasons, new vehicles, friends.

## 7. Social
Teams, crews, voice or chat chases, vehicle sharing. The rivalry is the content.

## 8. Packaging and performance
Car-forward icon. Large open map; performance on phones is a known cost (INFERRED).

## Numbers
- 8B visits, 173K peak, about 5K 7-day average in 2026 (RoWatcher/Profitable snippets).

## Mistakes to avoid
- Letting a flagship game age with no new onboarding. New players hit a world built for veterans.
- Slow cadence after a revamp: the spike decays in weeks.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: asymmetric teams, heists as modular content, visible vehicle status, map revamp as launch.
- Never copy: Jailbreak's map layout, vehicle designs, prison, the name or logo, or sound.
