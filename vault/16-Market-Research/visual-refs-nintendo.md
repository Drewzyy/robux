# Visual references: Nintendo and console classics

15 games. Pictures are in `attachments/game-refs/<id>/` once downloaded (BrewixGames: Make the Game, Knowledge, Game References, Download Pictures). Open the pictures with Read to look at them; they are other companies' art, for study only, never to copy or upload.

## Do this
- Pick 2 or 3 games below that match the game you are making, open their pictures, and name the exact thing you are borrowing (a button style, a silhouette rule, a map layout).
- Borrow rules, not art: shape, colour logic, layout and feedback. Never trace or reuse anyone's art, names or characters.
- Read the 'Steal this' line of each picked game first; it is the one rule worth copying.
- For shape language basics see [[16-Market-Research/visual-refs-shape-language]].

## Animal Crossing: New Horizons
`animal-crossing-new-horizons` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: ui, characters, world

- **UI:** NookPhone: every menu is an app icon on a phone; round, friendly and simple.
- **Characters and shapes:** Round heads, dot eyes, tiny bodies; villagers are animal shapes with one personality detail.
- **World:** A small island with rivers and cliffs you unlock tools to cross.
- **Steal this:** Phone-app home screen for menus; kids already understand it.

## Splatoon 3
`splatoon-3` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: ui, characters, world

- **UI:** Ink colours are the UI: the map shows team colours, and the result is a coverage percent.
- **Characters and shapes:** Inklings: squid-head silhouettes with bold streetwear.
- **World:** Urban arenas painted by play.
- **Steal this:** Let the world record what players did (paint, footprints).

## Splatoon 2
`splatoon-2` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Same ink-coverage UI.
- **Characters and shapes:** Inklings.
- **World:** Painted arenas.
- **Steal this:** Score as coverage percent.

## Mario Kart 8 Deluxe
`mario-kart-8` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Item roulette top-left, position number huge bottom-right.
- **Characters and shapes:** Mario cast in karts.
- **World:** Tracks with anti-gravity sections and bright themes.
- **Steal this:** Position number huge on screen; players always know where they stand.

## Super Mario Odyssey
`super-mario-odyssey` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: world

- **UI:** Moon count; minimal HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Mario and Cappy.
- **World:** Kingdoms dense with secrets, each a theme.
- **Steal this:** Pack every corner with a small reward.

## Super Mario Bros. Wonder
`super-mario-bros-wonder` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: characters, world

- **UI:** Badge selection UI.
- **Characters and shapes:** Elastic, expressive animation for every character.
- **World:** Levels that change wildly when you grab the Wonder Flower.
- **Steal this:** Hide a moment in each level where everything changes.

## Pokémon Scarlet and Violet
`pokemon-scarlet-violet` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: world

- **UI:** Map UI.
- **Characters and shapes:** Pokémon designs.
- **World:** Open region inspired by Spain and Portugal.
- **Steal this:** Base your world on a real place's feel.

## Kirby and the Forgotten Land
`kirby-forgotten-land` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: characters

- **UI:** Simple HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Kirby: a pink circle; Mouthful Mode wraps him around objects (a car, a cone) for comedy.
- **World:** Overgrown city ruins.
- **Steal this:** A circle hero can become anything; shape comedy.

## Pikmin 4
`pikmin-4` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: characters, world

- **UI:** Squad counts by colour.
- **Characters and shapes:** Tiny plant creatures, one colour per ability.
- **World:** Everyday places at tiny scale.
- **Steal this:** Colour equals ability for every unit.

## Super Smash Bros. Ultimate
`smash-ultimate` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Damage percent per player at the bottom with portraits.
- **Characters and shapes:** Many art styles side by side; each still readable.
- **World:** Stages with platforms.
- **Steal this:** Percent damage instead of health bars makes comebacks exciting.

## Astro Bot
`astro-bot` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: characters, world

- **UI:** Almost no HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Tiny white robot with a big head and blue eyes; dozens of cameo bots.
- **World:** Bright toy-like worlds.
- **Steal this:** A tiny cute robot hero with huge eyes.

## Luigi's Mansion 3
`luigis-mansion-3` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: world

- **UI:** Simple HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Ghosts with cute-spooky shapes.
- **World:** A hotel with a theme on every floor.
- **Steal this:** One building, a theme per floor.

## Super Mario Party Jamboree
`mario-party-jamboree` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: ui

- **UI:** Mode select as big colourful tiles.
- **Characters and shapes:** Mario cast.
- **World:** Boards and minigames.
- **Steal this:** Big tiles for each mode.

## Donkey Kong Bananza
`donkey-kong-bananza` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: world

- **UI:** Simple HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Expressive DK.
- **World:** Fully destructible voxel layers.
- **Steal this:** Destruction as the core fun.

## Minecraft (Wikipedia images)
`minecraft-wiki` · pictures from Wikipedia · good for: characters, world

- **UI:** Crafting grid.
- **Characters and shapes:** Mobs: Creeper and Enderman are textbook iconic silhouettes from cubes.
- **World:** Dimensions with their own palette.
- **Steal this:** Iconic monsters from basic cubes.
