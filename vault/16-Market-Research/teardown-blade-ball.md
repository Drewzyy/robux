# Teardown: Blade Ball

By Wiggity. A ball chases one player at a time. You press a button at the right moment to deflect it back. The ball speeds up and the last player standing wins.

## Do this
- Build a one-button skill with a rising difficulty curve. Anyone can learn deflecting in 10 seconds; mastery takes weeks.
- Use a single homing threat that targets one player at a time. Everyone else watches the tension, so spectating is fun.
- Keep rounds short (a few minutes) with a quick restart.
- Add abilities (a short list of equippable powers) and swords as visible cosmetics. They are what you sell.
- Add a ranked mode with a visible rank so skilled players have a ladder.
- Run battle-pass seasons with finisher effects and cosmetic rewards. Keep the core mechanic equal for everyone.
- Build a Discord community for patch news (the developer's Discord reportedly passed 500K members; UNVERIFIED).

## 1. Identity
- Developer: Wiggity. About 6.04B visits (RoWatcher/Profitable snippets). Peak: RoWatcher lists 46K on 2026-09-20 with about 10K live and a 17K average; Naavik reported 418K (older). UNVERIFIED which is right.
- Update cadence: seasonal passes and ability batches. Exact days UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Core loop
Spawn, track the ball, parry on time, outlast others, win, earn currency, equip abilities and swords. Verb every 5 s: watch and press. Reward every 30 s: survive another parry.

## 3. Progression
Ranks, abilities, sword skins, battle pass tiers, crates. Content is cosmetic and ability variety.

## 4. Monetization
Abilities, weapon skins, battle passes, finisher effects (Naavik snippet). Third-party monthly estimate: about $106K, moderate confidence. A 2024 newsletter said it avoids aggressive monetization yet ranked highly in earnings. Naavik argues competitive Roblox games tolerate pay-to-win and loot boxes, which is a legal and trust risk for us: check [[07-Monetization/paid-random-items-checklist]].

## 5. First 60 seconds (INFERRED)
Queue into a lobby, 20 to 30 seconds of spawn, a ball launches, you press parry. First success likely within 30 s.

## 6. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: friends and clips of clutch parries, a one-sentence mechanic.
- Stay: skill improves visibly; ranks reward practice.
- Return: new abilities, seasons and rank resets, friends.

## 7. Social
Lobbies, spectating, leaderboards, a big Discord. Competitive social.

## 8. Packaging and performance
A blade and ball thumbnail. Simple arena, low part count, runs on phones (INFERRED).

## Numbers
- 6.04B visits, 46K peak on one tracker, 418K in an older report. Treat as range.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Ability imbalance. Overpowered paid abilities cause quit waves.
- Latency: a timing game needs a stable server. Plan for client prediction.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: one-button skill, single tracked threat, short rounds, cosmetic monetization, ranked ladder.
- Never copy: the Blade Ball name, ball and sword art, abilities, arena or UI.
