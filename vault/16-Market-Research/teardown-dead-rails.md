# Teardown: Dead Rails

A co-op survival game where a train carries the group west across a hostile Wild West. Players loot, fight undead and bandits, and keep the train fuelled to reach the end.

## Do this
- Make a run a journey with a clear distance and a final point. A map with a start and end gives a shareable "I finished it".
- Add a roguelike loss: death ends the run, and some currency carries over. Each run is a short story.
- Let players split roles (loot, defend, drive). Co-op roles make friends useful to each other.
- Make resources scarce on the route so decisions matter (spend or save).
- Add an endless or high-score mode after launch to extend the tail. Dead Rails ran an "Endless" variant and event variants (Heatwave, a ghost event) on the same ID.
- Plan servers for a sudden crowd. Roblox's newsroom noted Dead Rails used all available CPU capacity when it spiked to 1M. Reduce server cost per player before launch.
- Plan the decay. Keep a content roadmap for the month after the spike, not only launch week.

## 1. Identity
- Spike: 1M concurrent in March 2025 (Roblox newsroom, June 2025).
- Later: RoWatcher shows about 11K now, a 15K 7-day average, down 38% over 30 days, with an all-time peak of 79K on 2025-11-28 on that page. Profitable shows 19.5K for the Endless variant on 2026-07-07. Last update June 16, 2026 (Rotrends).
- Update cadence: event variants and updates; slower in 2026. Developer name UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Core loop
Board the train, loot stops, fight, keep fuel and defences up, reach the next stage, bank currency. Verb every 5 s: shoot, loot, or fuel. Reward every 30 s: loot, a kill, a distance marker.

## 3. Progression
Bonds or currency, weapons and gear unlocks, run counters, an endless mode. Detail UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Monetization
UNVERIFIED. Typical (general knowledge): gamepasses for gear and convenience. Not read in session.

## 5. First 60 seconds (INFERRED)
Lobby into a train, quick loot at the first stop, the first enemy within a minute.

## 6. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: a viral clip, a Wild West aesthetic, friends.
- Stay: a run is a story; a bad run teaches.
- Return: event variants, endless mode, new weapons. The data suggests this was not enough: the player count fell by 90 percent or more from the spike.

## 7. Social
Co-op squads, role play, creator content.

## 8. Packaging and performance
Wild West train thumbnail. Heavy server load per player in spike (Roblox newsroom).

## Numbers
- 1M CCU spike, March 2025; 11K to 19.5K in mid-2026.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Treating a spike as a base. Plan for a decay to about 1 to 5 percent of peak within a year.
- Launching events on separate IDs that split the audience.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: a journey with an end, a roguelike loss, role-based co-op, an endless mode.
- Never copy: the Dead Rails name, train, enemies or map. Use a different setting.
