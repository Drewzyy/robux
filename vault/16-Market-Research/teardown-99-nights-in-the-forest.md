# Teardown: 99 Nights in the Forest

Players are stranded in a forest. They must survive 99 nights, gather resources, rescue lost children, and keep a campfire alive against creatures that come at night. By Grandma's Favourite Games, which runs about 15 games.

## Do this
- State the win condition in the title (99 nights). A countdown to a clear end creates a long, shared goal.
- Build the day as gather, the night as defend. The cycle gives a rhythm players can predict and plan around.
- Make the shared fire (or base) the thing everyone protects. Shared stakes force co-op.
- Give each night a new threat or change, so night 10 differs from night 50.
- Reward rescuing or collecting named characters (children in this game) as a permanent collection across runs.
- Use a death penalty that sends players back to a lobby but keeps some meta progress, so a loss still earns something.
- Make a run long enough to be an event (a full 99 nights is hours) but allow short sessions via checkpoints or partial goals. UNVERIFIED whether this game does.

## 1. Identity
- Developer: Grandma's Favourite Games. Peak 14.2M concurrent in 2025 (PC Gamer). One snapshot has 288,558 live; another cites 219K and 25.4B visits. Late Oct 2026: 146K (StudioKrew).
- Update cadence: recurring content drops, UNVERIFIED. A swarm of copycats exists (Steal a 99 Nights and similar), a sign of how far it spread.

## 2. Core loop
Explore by day for wood, food and tools, return to the camp before dark, fuel the fire, survive the night, rescue children, craft upgrades, repeat. Verb every 5 s: chop, pick up, or fight. Reward every 30 s: a resource or craft.

## 3. Progression
Items, tools, base upgrades, a run-long night counter, and meta unlocks. Rescued children and cosmetics act as collection. Detail UNVERIFIED.

## 5. First 60 seconds (INFERRED)
Spawn in a forest near a campfire, a quick objective (gather wood), an ambient scare, an early night. The player sees the "Night 1 of 99" counter right away.

## 6. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: scary-but-friendly horror clips, a title with a number, a co-op invite.
- Stay: a long run with friends, shared success at dawn.
- Return: new events and content, the 99-night challenge, new monsters, the creator community.

## 7. Social
Small squads working to the same goal. Server hopping for different groups. Creator videos drive discovery.

## 8. Packaging and performance
Night-forest thumbnail with a flashlight. Open terrain can be heavy; mobile performance UNVERIFIED.

## Numbers
- 14.2M peak (2025), 146K (2026-10-29), 25.4B visits (one tracker).

## Mistakes to avoid
- Too much grind between nights. Players leave if a night feels like a chore.
- Relying on one viral month. The game fell by about 99% from peak, so plan the long tail.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: a numbered goal, a day/night cycle, a shared base, collectible rescues.
- Never copy: the title pattern "99 Nights", the creatures, the child characters, the map or audio. Do not make "Steal a 99 Nights" style clones.
