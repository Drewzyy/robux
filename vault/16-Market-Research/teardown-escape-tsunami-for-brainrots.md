# Teardown: Escape Tsunami For Brainrots

Released December 15, 2025 by the "Wave of Brainrots" group. Players collect valuable characters while running ahead of a recurring tsunami across a track, then carry them to safety.

## Do this
- Put a repeating threat on a timer. A wave arrives every few seconds to minutes, so every moment has pressure and a clear "go now" or "wait" choice.
- Make the reward something you carry, so greed has a visible cost. Pick up more, run slower, risk more.
- Use a track layout with distance zones. Further means rarer and riskier, which gives a built-in progression map.
- Let players bank their haul at a safe point, with a visible counter of what they saved.
- Keep a run under 2 minutes so death costs little and a retry is instant.
- Add a rarity ladder with themed tiers so new content is a new tier, not a new system.
- Launch near a hot trend and add your own mechanic. Do not clone the trend's name; the clone ecosystem around this title is large (dozens of "Escape X for Brainrots" games).

## 1. Identity
- Released 2025-12-15. 5.2B to 5.7B visits depending on snapshot.
- CCU: 71,835 on one tracker, about 140K in a news piece, 5.3K on another page, and a list saying it frequently passes 600K. These are snapshots at different times, so use only as a range: about 5K to 600K. Peak UNVERIFIED.
- Update cadence: UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Core loop
Pick up a character, run along the track avoiding the wave, bank it, buy better gear or speed, go further next run. Verb every 5 s: run, jump, grab. Reward every 30 s: a pick-up or a bank.

## 3. Progression, monetization
Speed upgrades, rarity tiers, rebirths (typical, UNVERIFIED). Not read. Any random hatch must pass [[07-Monetization/paid-random-items-checklist]].

## 4. First 60 seconds (INFERRED)
Spawn at the start, a short tutorial run, the first wave arrives in under 30 seconds.

## 5. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: a title that says the whole game; the meme wave.
- Stay: faster runs and rarer carries.
- Return: new waves, events, friends racing.

## 6. Social
Public servers, rescue runs, creator content.

## Numbers
- 5.2B to 5.7B visits in about 11 months. Peak UNVERIFIED.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Reskin churn. Many copies at under 10K CCU show that a template alone does not retain (compare [[16-Market-Research/teardown-plus1-speed]]).
- Heavy waves on phones: test mobile frame rate.

## Open questions for our own research
- Confirm the all-time peak on two trackers dated the same hour.
- Read the shop for prices and odds before copying any paid item.
- Measure wave timing in the live game (interval and speed) and write it down with the date.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: a timed hazard plus a carried reward plus a short run.
- Never copy: the name, meme characters, wave art, audio or the "Escape X For Brainrots" title pattern.
