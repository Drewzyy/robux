# Visual references: Roblox simulators and tycoons

17 games. Pictures are in `attachments/game-refs/<id>/` once downloaded (BrewixGames: Make the Game, Knowledge, Game References, Download Pictures). Open the pictures with Read to look at them; they are other companies' art, for study only, never to copy or upload.

## Do this
- Pick 2 or 3 games below that match the game you are making, open their pictures, and name the exact thing you are borrowing (a button style, a silhouette rule, a map layout).
- Borrow rules, not art: shape, colour logic, layout and feedback. Never trace or reuse anyone's art, names or characters.
- Read the 'Steal this' line of each picked game first; it is the one rule worth copying.
- For shape language basics see [[16-Market-Research/visual-refs-shape-language]].

## Pet Simulator 99
`pet-simulator-99` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Huge bold numbers float off every coin pile; currency counters sit top-left in chunky rounded pills with icons bigger than the text.
- **Characters and shapes:** Pets are a sphere-plus-ears silhouette: one round body, two features, so 100s read at 40 px. Rarity is told by glow and outline, not shape.
- **World:** Zones are flat tiled areas with a single colour theme each and a gate you pay to open; the next zone is always visible through the gate.
- **Steal this:** Make every collectible readable from its silhouette at icon size, then use colour and glow for rarity tiers.

## Bee Swarm Simulator
`bee-swarm-simulator` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, world

- **UI:** Hive and bag meters are big bars at the top; the quest log is a scrolling list of plain sentences with progress numbers.
- **Characters and shapes:** Bees are one circle plus two wing ovals; each type changes only colour and a face mark, so the swarm reads as a crowd.
- **World:** Fields are flat coloured patches sized by level (bigger, higher fields later), arranged around a central hub with mountains you climb.
- **Steal this:** Lay out progression as physical height: later areas are literally higher up and visible from the start.

## Grow a Garden
`grow-a-garden` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Shop restocks on a visible clock; the seed shop is a big framed card grid with stock counts and a rarity colour bar.
- **Characters and shapes:** Players are plain avatars; the plants are the stars, with mutations shown by colour, sparkles and size.
- **World:** Each player gets an identical fenced plot in a row along one road, so everyone can see everyone's garden.
- **Steal this:** Put every player's progress where others can see it from the spawn path; envy sells better than ads.

## Bubble Gum Simulator INFINITY
`bubble-gum-simulator-infinity` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Giant rounded buttons on the left edge with icon plus one word; hatch animations fill the whole screen with a burst and rarity banner.
- **Characters and shapes:** Pets are chubby, eyes high on the face, very short limbs; 'shiny' and 'mythic' get rainbow shaders, not new shapes.
- **World:** Worlds stack vertically as floating islands; you rise through them by blowing a bigger bubble.
- **Steal this:** Celebrate a rare hatch with a full-screen moment and a server-wide message.

## Bubble Gum Simulator (original)
`bubble-gum-simulator` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Classic 2019 simulator HUD: left column of square buttons, coin counters top, egg prices floating over each egg.
- **Characters and shapes:** Same chubby pet style as the newer game; compare both to see how the HUD got cleaner.
- **World:** Islands floating above a base world; height is progress.
- **Steal this:** Show the price of every egg in the world, above the egg, not inside a menu.

## Theme Park Tycoon 2
`theme-park-tycoon-2` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Building toolbar along the bottom with category tabs, like a PC builder game; money and park rating pinned top.
- **Characters and shapes:** Guests are small and many; their mood shows as bubbles above heads.
- **World:** A big empty grass plot per player; the fun is the blank canvas plus a long list of rides.
- **Steal this:** Give builders a bottom toolbar with categories, a grid snap, and an undo; that is the whole genre.

## Lumber Tycoon 2
`lumber-tycoon-2` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world

- **UI:** Almost no HUD; money and a tiny inventory. Teaches through the world itself.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars; trees and axes carry the art.
- **World:** One large handmade map with biomes far apart (snow, volcano, swamp), each holding a rare wood; travel is part of the progression.
- **Steal this:** Hide rare rewards at the far ends of a big map so players tell each other where to go.

## Restaurant Tycoon 2
`restaurant-tycoon-2` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Cozy cream and wood UI panels; build menu with clear category icons and price tags.
- **Characters and shapes:** Customers are generic; staff uniforms show role by colour.
- **World:** Each player builds inside a lot on a shared street, so visitors can walk in and eat.
- **Steal this:** Let other players physically visit and use what someone built.

## Military Tycoon
`military-tycoon` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Classic tycoon buttons on the floor with price and name, plus a sleek shop overlay with vehicle cards.
- **Characters and shapes:** Vehicles are the characters: chunky, readable silhouettes (tank, jet, heli) at any distance.
- **World:** Bases around a shared battlefield; every base faces the middle.
- **Steal this:** Tycoon pads on the floor: price in big text, a colour that shows can-afford vs can't.

## Steal a Brainrot
`steal-a-brainrot` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, thumbnail

- **UI:** Huge income numbers over each base; a moving conveyor of items to buy with rarity labels floating above.
- **Characters and shapes:** Meme characters with very loud silhouettes and colours, each instantly nameable.
- **World:** Bases in two rows facing a central conveyor, so stealing and defending happen in plain view.
- **Steal this:** Put the risk and the reward on the same screen: everyone can see what you own and come take it.

## Fisch
`fisch` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** The reel minigame is one bar with a moving zone; everything else stays hidden while fishing.
- **Characters and shapes:** Fish art is large, detailed and bestiary-style; the player is small.
- **World:** Islands with distinct weather and music, each with its own rare fish.
- **Steal this:** One simple skill minigame done many thousands of times; tune its feel before anything else.

## PLS DONATE
`pls-donate` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Each booth shows a big sign with raised amount; donation popups are bright and huge.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars; the booth is the character.
- **World:** A circle of identical booths around a plaza.
- **Steal this:** Social proof made physical: show the total next to each player.

## Sol's RNG
`sols-rng` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, thumbnail

- **UI:** One big Roll button; the rolled aura's name and odds (1 in X) fill the screen with a cutscene for rare rolls.
- **Characters and shapes:** Auras are particle effects around a plain avatar; rarity equals more particles, light and motion.
- **World:** A small hub; the roll is the game.
- **Steal this:** Print the odds ('1 in 1,000,000') on rare rewards; the number itself is the flex.

## Anime Defenders
`anime-defenders` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Unit cards along the bottom with cost and cooldown; upgrade panel opens beside the unit, not over the map.
- **Characters and shapes:** Small units with exaggerated heads and weapons, readable from top-down.
- **World:** Lane maps with one path, viewed from high above.
- **Steal this:** In tower defense keep the map clear: menus open beside, never over, the action.

## Toilet Tower Defense
`toilet-tower-defense` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, thumbnail

- **UI:** Bright card frames per rarity; summon banners with a timer.
- **Characters and shapes:** Meme units with one loud silhouette each (camera heads, speaker heads).
- **World:** Short lanes with a few bends, readable at a glance.
- **Steal this:** A meme can carry a genre if each unit is instantly recognisable.

## Anime Adventures
`anime-adventures` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Dense UI aimed at older players: unit grids, traits, stats; useful as a 'how much is too much' example.
- **Characters and shapes:** Units with big hair shapes and strong colour accents.
- **World:** Lane maps themed on anime places.
- **Steal this:** Compare to Anime Defenders to see how much clutter a young audience will tolerate.

## Islands
`islands` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, ui

- **UI:** Inventory and crafting panels similar to Minecraft but rounder and softer.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars; resources and machines carry the look.
- **World:** Your own floating island, plus shared hub islands for trading and bosses.
- **Steal this:** Give each player a private home that grows, and a shared place to show it off.
