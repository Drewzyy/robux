# Reviews: fighting and PvP games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Match by skill or level; stomping new players is the top PvP quit cause (UNVERIFIED general pattern from player reviews and forum threads; no niche-specific counted study found.)
- Add hit feedback: hit stop 50-80 ms, sound, screen shake, damage numbers.
- Server-validate every hit and move; rate limit remotes against exploiters.
- Offer weapons sold for Robux with equal stats to free ones, or cosmetic only.
- Show a clear loss reason (kill cam or damage log).
- Rebalance with patch notes every 2 weeks.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. Crisp hit feel. Phrasing: 'every hit feels powerful.'
2. Skill expression. Phrasing: 'I got better and beat tough players.'
3. Unlocking moves and styles.
4. Short rounds and quick rematches.
5. Ranked ladders and titles.
6. Fights with friends.

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Pay to win weapons or fruits.
2. Exploiters and hackers.
3. Matches with much stronger players.
4. Spawn camping and stun lock.
5. Lag and desync on hits.
6. Balance changes that make favorite builds useless.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- Skill or level matchmaking.
- Server hit validation.
- Spawn protection of 3 seconds.
- Stun lock diminishing returns.
- Public patch notes.

### Never do this
- Never sell stat advantages.
- Never trust the client for damage.
- Never let new players face max level ones.
- Never copy another game's move names or animations.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] Matchmaking tested with mixed levels.
2. [ ] Hit validation server side.
3. [ ] Remote rate limits.
4. [ ] Spawn protection.
5. [ ] Stun lock cap.
6. [ ] Free and paid weapon parity.
7. [ ] Kill cam.
8. [ ] Ping 150 ms fairness test.
9. [ ] Patch note template.
10. [ ] Report cheater button.
