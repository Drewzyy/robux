# Visual references: Roblox roleplay, social and fashion

10 games. Pictures are in `attachments/game-refs/<id>/` once downloaded (BrewixGames: Make the Game, Knowledge, Game References, Download Pictures). Open the pictures with Read to look at them; they are other companies' art, for study only, never to copy or upload.

## Do this
- Pick 2 or 3 games below that match the game you are making, open their pictures, and name the exact thing you are borrowing (a button style, a silhouette rule, a map layout).
- Borrow rules, not art: shape, colour logic, layout and feedback. Never trace or reuse anyone's art, names or characters.
- Read the 'Steal this' line of each picked game first; it is the one rule worth copying.
- For shape language basics see [[16-Market-Research/visual-refs-shape-language]].

## Adopt Me!
`adopt-me` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Rounded, pastel buttons with thick dark outlines and icons first; money in a pill at the top. Very young-player friendly.
- **Characters and shapes:** Pets: oversized heads, big eyes set low, tiny bodies; neon and mega versions glow instead of changing shape.
- **World:** A small town with clear landmarks (school, hospital, nursery) around a central plaza; every building is a one-word idea.
- **Steal this:** Thick outlines and pastel fills on every button; one icon per button, and text only when needed.

## Brookhaven RP
`brookhaven` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Minimal UI: a few round side buttons for houses, cars and avatar edits; the town is the interface.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars; roleplay comes from outfits and props.
- **World:** A walkable town on a grid with houses, a school, a police station and a hospital; every building is a roleplay prompt.
- **Steal this:** In roleplay, each building is a suggestion of a story; give the map recognisable real-life places.

## Welcome to Bloxburg
`bloxburg` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** The build mode UI is the best on Roblox: category tabs, item cards with price, rotation and colour tools; study it for any building game.
- **Characters and shapes:** Realistic-ish avatars; moods are bars and icons.
- **World:** A suburban town with jobs as places (pizza shop, supermarket); your house is your lot.
- **Steal this:** Copy its build mode: category tabs, grid with snap, a colour picker on every item.

## Royale High
`royale-high` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Sparkly pastel UI with gold trim; outfit menus are big card grids with layering.
- **Characters and shapes:** Very tall, slim, stylised avatars with wings, halos and huge skirts; accessories are the progression.
- **World:** A castle school campus with themed realms, dreamy skyboxes and lots of pink and lilac.
- **Steal this:** For fashion games, the outfit preview must be big, centred and rotatable.

## Livetopia RP
`livetopia` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Large circular side buttons, bright and simple, for very young players.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars with lots of free props and pets.
- **World:** A colourful town much like Brookhaven, with more theme-park style places.
- **Steal this:** Bright round buttons with one icon each are the safe default for ages 6-10.

## Dress To Impress
`dress-to-impress` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, thumbnail

- **UI:** Clothing racks in the world, a big runway voting screen with stars; the theme of each round is shown huge at the top.
- **Characters and shapes:** Models with long legs and simple faces so outfits are the focus; hair and makeup are big categories.
- **World:** A dressing room around a runway stage; everything points to the stage.
- **Steal this:** Give each round one theme word shown huge, then a vote; a simple social loop.

## Work at a Pizza Place
`work-at-a-pizza-place` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, ui

- **UI:** Almost no UI; jobs are taught by the objects (a dough station, an oven, a car).
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars in job hats.
- **World:** A small town where the shop is the centre and houses ring it.
- **Steal this:** Teach with objects in the world, not text: a station's shape says what it does.

## MIC UP
`mic-up` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world

- **UI:** Almost no UI; voice chat is the game.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars; emotes and dances.
- **World:** A hangout map with stages, seats and photo spots.
- **Steal this:** A hangout needs places to sit, stand on stage and pose; that is the content.

## Greenville RP
`greenville` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, ui

- **UI:** Phone UI for jobs and cars.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars; cars are the stars.
- **World:** A realistic small American town with roads designed for driving roleplay.
- **Steal this:** Real-life road layouts make driving roleplay feel right.

## Emergency Response: Liberty County
`emergency-response-liberty-county` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Team select screen with police, fire, EMS and criminal roles; radio and callout UI.
- **Characters and shapes:** Uniforms by team; vehicles with lights.
- **World:** A county map with stations and hotspots for calls.
- **Steal this:** Team roles with clear uniforms give roleplay structure.
