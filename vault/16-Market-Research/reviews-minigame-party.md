# Reviews: minigame and party games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Rounds of 60-120 seconds with a 15 second intermission.
- At least 15 different minigames at launch; repeat no game twice in a row.
- Everyone plays: no early elimination longer than 30 seconds without spectate fun.
- Let players vote on the next game.
- Show points live and end with a podium.
- Test with 8 to 16 players.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. Variety.
2. Laughs with friends.
3. Quick rounds.
4. Fair chance for new players.
5. Silly rewards.
6. Easy rules.

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Same minigame repeated.
2. Waiting after elimination.
3. Unfair or laggy rounds.
4. Toxic chat.
5. Rigged voting.
6. Pay perks that win rounds.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- No-repeat shuffler.
- Spectator mini-activity.
- Vote UI.
- Ranked podium.
- Chat filter.

### Never do this
- Never sell round-winning perks.
- Never let elimination idle over 30 seconds.
- Never copy named TV show formats' art.
- Never repeat a minigame back to back.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] 15 games.
2. [ ] Shuffler.
3. [ ] Spectate fun.
4. [ ] Vote.
5. [ ] Podium.
6. [ ] Chat filter.
7. [ ] 16 player test.
8. [ ] Intermission 15 seconds.
9. [ ] Cosmetic shop only.
10. [ ] New game monthly.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: give players the loved things above for free and early, and remove the hated things before launch, especially unfair pay walls, hidden odds and bugs in the first 10 minutes.
- Never copy names, characters, art, music, logos or UI skins from any game named here. Keep prompts to buy player-initiated and show odds and prices plainly, since our audience includes children.
