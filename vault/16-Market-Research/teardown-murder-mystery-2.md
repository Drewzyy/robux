# Teardown: Murder Mystery 2

By Nikilis. Each round, one player is secretly the murderer, one is the sheriff with a gun, everyone else is innocent. The murderer must kill all before time runs out.

## Do this
- Use a 3-role secret assignment each round (one killer, one armed player, the rest innocent). Roles give three different play styles inside one map.
- Keep a round to a few minutes with an instant new round after, so the cost of dying is a short wait.
- Make the collectible the weapon skin: it is visible in every round, so owning one is a status signal other players see.
- Sell the skin through crates and direct purchase, and let them be traded only after a level gate (MM2 requires level 10 since Dec 2021, with four unique items per side per trade).
- Do not publish an official value list unless you can maintain it; the community builds its own and the developer takes no liability. UNVERIFIED whether this is wise.
- Update with a themed event and new item batch every few weeks to keep the economy fresh.
- Add a spectator view for dead players, so waiting is not boring.

## 1. Identity
- Developer: Nikilis. About 24.6B visits after 12 years (RoWatcher headline, 2026).
- CCU: 538.7K on 2026-08-20 (StudioKrew), 473K on 2026-10-29 (StudioKrew snippet), 433K and a 7-day average of 235K on another tracker page, all-time peak 1.4M on 2026-08-16 (RoWatcher). Trackers disagree on dates and times.
- Update cadence: regular seasonal updates (Halloween, winter, etc. general knowledge). Exact days UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Core loop
Join a round, receive a role, play it, earn coins, buy crates and skins, show them off next round. Verb every 5 s: move, hide, or aim. Reward every 30 s: survive, spot the killer, or kill.

## 3. Progression
Level, coins, weapon rarity tiers (the top tier being "godly"), perks, collections. Trading adds a second economy.

## 4. Monetization
Rotrends lists 3 gamepasses and 136 developer products: it leans on developer products (crates, bundles). Individual prices UNVERIFIED. Random crates fall under [[07-Monetization/paid-random-items-checklist]].

## 5. First 60 seconds (INFERRED)
Lobby, round timer, role card, play. First death or win within about a minute.

## 6. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: friends invite you, it is free, the rule fits in a sentence.
- Stay: every round differs because the other players change.
- Return: new weapons, trading, events, and a status inventory.

## 7. Social
Trading with confirmation, group chat during rounds, a large off-platform market (value sites, Discord). Third-party selling of items exists and may break Roblox rules, so treat it as a risk, not a feature.

## 8. Packaging and performance
Simple, readable maps. Runs on low-end phones (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED).

## Numbers
- 24.6B visits, 1.4M peak, 233K-538K typical across 2026 snapshots.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Opening trades without level gates or limits. Scams and account-theft attempts follow.
- Letting the value of items be set only off-platform, which invites black-market selling.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: hidden-role rounds, visible collectible, level-gated trading, slow steady updates.
- Never copy: Murder Mystery 2's name, knife and gun designs, godly list, maps, UI, or audio.
