# Teardown: Dress to Impress

A round-based fashion contest. Each round has a theme, players dress up in a few minutes, walk a runway, and others rate them. Built by a teen developer and later run alongside a larger group (the snippets differ on who; UNVERIFIED).

## Do this
- Use short rounds: a theme, a dress-up timer (about 3 to 5 minutes), a runway, scores, repeat. Each round resets the stakes.
- Let players express themselves, not progress. The main reward is being rated, so give a visible score or crowd reaction at the end of each runway walk.
- Make the theme the content. A new theme each round gives infinite variety without new assets.
- Use brand and creator collaborations as events (a Lady Gaga integration drew 591,448 concurrent and about 90 million visits in a week, per Gamefam and RoWatcher).
- Sell wardrobe items and a VIP tier that change how you look, not who wins. Keep the vote a human judgment.
- Add a free daily reward so free players keep up in looks.
- Add a report and mute flow for judging chat. Child safety matters in a rating game.

## 1. Identity
- Visits: about 9.7B (RoWatcher headline, 2026). Roblox group ownership and launch date: UNVERIFIED.
- CCU: 591,448 during the Lady Gaga event (RoWatcher/search summary). 250K+ average in Sep 2024 (one article). 128.2K online in a mid-2026 tracker snapshot. A "1.5M peak" claim is UNVERIFIED and likely wrong.
- Update cadence: frequent seasonal drops and collabs. Exact days UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Core loop
Join a round, get a theme, build an outfit from a large wardrobe, walk the runway, get rated, win in-game currency, buy more items. Verb every 5 s: pick an item. Reward every 30 s: a visible outfit change. Session goal: win a round. Weekly goal: a new item set from the season.

## 3. Progression
Cash for clothes, unlocks for rarer items, level and rank for votes. Scarcity: seasonal items and VIP-only categories. Collection of outfits is the long hold.

## 4. Monetization
Gamepasses (VIP, extra slots, premium categories) and developer products for currency. Prices UNVERIFIED. Full list not read.

## 5. First 60 seconds (INFERRED)
Spawn in a lobby, queue joins a round, a theme banner appears, the timer starts, the wardrobe opens. First visible success (a styled avatar) comes in the first 30 s.

## 6. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: friends and creators post outfits, it looks good in a vertical clip, and you do not need to be good at a game.
- Stay: rounds are short and different each time; being rated feels personal.
- Return: new themes, collabs, seasonal items, and showing friends.

## 7. Social
Judging by other players, runway audience, friends in the same round, a creator content loop. This is the strongest retention driver.

## 8. Packaging and performance
Icon is a stylised outfit. Heavy avatar and accessory loading can hurt low-end phones (INFERRED).

## Numbers
- 9.7B visits, 591,448 event CCU, about 128K typical mid-2026.
- Gamefam says one collab produced about 90M visits in a week.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Rating loops with no moderation. Report tools are required.
- Selling items that decide the winner (pay-to-win in a vote erodes trust).

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: themed short rounds, social rating, content-as-theme, collab events, cosmetic monetization.
- Never copy: the DTI name, runway map, UI, item catalog, music, or any real brand or celebrity likeness. Do not use licensed IP; zero-spend and no paid assets.
