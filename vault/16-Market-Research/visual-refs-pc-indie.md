# Visual references: Indie and PC favourites

29 games. Pictures are in `attachments/game-refs/<id>/` once downloaded (BrewixGames: Make the Game, Knowledge, Game References, Download Pictures). Open the pictures with Read to look at them; they are other companies' art, for study only, never to copy or upload.

## Do this
- Pick 2 or 3 games below that match the game you are making, open their pictures, and name the exact thing you are borrowing (a button style, a silhouette rule, a map layout).
- Borrow rules, not art: shape, colour logic, layout and feedback. Never trace or reuse anyone's art, names or characters.
- Read the 'Steal this' line of each picked game first; it is the one rule worth copying.
- For shape language basics see [[16-Market-Research/visual-refs-shape-language]].

## Hades
`hades` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, characters, world

- **UI:** Boon choices are three large framed cards with a god's colour and portrait; you always know whose gift it is.
- **Characters and shapes:** Every character has a huge readable silhouette and its own colour (Zeus gold, Poseidon teal); portraits are painted, bold and expressive.
- **World:** Chambers are small arenas seen from an angled top view, with a clear exit door that previews the reward.
- **Steal this:** Show the reward on the door before players choose a path.

## Hades II
`hades-ii` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Same card-choice UI evolved; compare with Hades to see how they kept it familiar.
- **Characters and shapes:** Painted portraits, strong colour per god.
- **World:** Angled top-down arenas.
- **Steal this:** Keep a UI players already learned; change the content, not the controls.

## Hollow Knight
`hollow-knight` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, characters, world

- **UI:** Health is a row of masks top-left, soul in a round vessel; tiny, elegant, never in the way.
- **Characters and shapes:** The knight is a white mask with two horns on a black cloak: three shapes, instantly iconic.
- **World:** Each area has one dominant colour (Greenpath is green, the City of Tears is blue and rainy), so you always know where you are.
- **Steal this:** Give each zone one dominant colour so the map is readable from a screenshot.

## Hollow Knight: Silksong
`hollow-knight-silksong` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, world

- **UI:** Same minimal HUD style.
- **Characters and shapes:** Hornet: needle, cloak and a horned mask; a tall, sharp triangle silhouette versus the round Knight.
- **World:** Layered painted backgrounds with depth fog.
- **Steal this:** Triangle shapes for agile, sharp heroes; round shapes for cute ones.

## Celeste
`celeste` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, world

- **UI:** Almost no HUD; strawberries counted only at screen transitions.
- **Characters and shapes:** Madeline's hair colour shows if her dash is ready (red ready, blue used): character design as UI.
- **World:** One screen per challenge, each teaching one idea before testing it.
- **Steal this:** Show ability state on the character itself, not in a HUD.

## Cuphead
`cuphead` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, world

- **UI:** Health as a card in the corner; super meter as cards filling.
- **Characters and shapes:** 1930s rubber-hose style: round bodies, pie-cut eyes, bendy limbs; every boss has phases with new silhouettes.
- **World:** Hand-painted backgrounds with layered parallax.
- **Steal this:** A strong art era (1930s cartoons) gives instant identity; pick a style and commit.

## Stardew Valley
`stardew-valley` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Cozy wood-framed panels, clock and calendar top-right; the energy bar is the day's budget.
- **Characters and shapes:** Small pixel villagers with clear portraits for dialogue.
- **World:** A farm plus a small town with seasons changing the palette.
- **Steal this:** Use seasons or events to recolour the same map; it feels new for little work.

## Terraria
`terraria` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Inventory grid always one key away; hotbar top-left.
- **Characters and shapes:** Small sprites; bosses huge by comparison for drama.
- **World:** A side-on world with clear layers (surface, caves, underworld) each getting harder.
- **Steal this:** Make deeper or farther equal harder, so players read danger from position.

## Dead Cells
`dead-cells` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, characters, world

- **UI:** Weapon slots bottom with cooldown fills; item stats shown as colour scrolls.
- **Characters and shapes:** The hero's head is a flame: a unique, simple, readable idea.
- **World:** Biomes with strong palettes and clear exits.
- **Steal this:** Give the hero one memorable shape idea (a flame head, a mask).

## Balatro
`balatro` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui

- **UI:** Numbers pop and shake as scores add up; chips times mult is shown as two big coloured boxes. Pure UI juice.
- **Characters and shapes:** Cards and jokers with bold flat art.
- **World:** Felt table with a slow swirling background.
- **Steal this:** Make the scoring moment shake, pop and count up; that is where the dopamine is.

## Vampire Survivors
`vampire-survivors` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui

- **UI:** Level-up choice is three items in a box; XP bar across the top; gems flood the screen.
- **Characters and shapes:** Tiny sprites; the screen full of enemies is the spectacle.
- **World:** Flat endless fields.
- **Steal this:** Flood the screen with pickups; collecting many small things feels great.

## Slay the Spire
`slay-the-spire` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui

- **UI:** Enemy intent icons above each enemy tell you what they will do next turn.
- **Characters and shapes:** Bold, simple monster designs.
- **World:** A branching map you can see in full before choosing.
- **Steal this:** Show the enemy's next move as an icon; it turns guessing into strategy.

## Cult of the Lamb
`cult-of-the-lamb` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, world, ui

- **UI:** Clean bold UI with a red crown motif; follower needs as icons over heads.
- **Characters and shapes:** Cute round animals with big heads and dark themes: cute plus creepy.
- **World:** Base building plus dungeon runs in contrasting palettes.
- **Steal this:** Cute shapes can carry dark themes for older kids.

## Dave the Diver
`dave-the-diver` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** A phone UI inside the game hosts menus; oxygen bar is the timer.
- **Characters and shapes:** Chunky pixel characters with expressive cutscenes.
- **World:** A day loop: dive by day, serve sushi by night.
- **Steal this:** Two loops that feed each other (catch then sell).

## A Short Hike
`a-short-hike` · pictures from Steam · good for: world, characters

- **UI:** Feathers top-left are both stamina and progress.
- **Characters and shapes:** Small bird characters with simple shapes.
- **World:** One mountain island you can see from everywhere; the peak is the goal.
- **Steal this:** Put the goal in view from the start (a peak, a tower).

## TUNIC
`tunic` · pictures from Steam · good for: world, characters

- **UI:** The manual is an in-game item you collect page by page.
- **Characters and shapes:** A tiny fox in a green tunic; heroic but small.
- **World:** Isometric world full of hidden paths behind walls.
- **Steal this:** Hide secrets players can see but not reach yet.

## Pizza Tower
`pizza-tower` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, ui

- **UI:** TV in the corner shows the hero's face reacting to what happens.
- **Characters and shapes:** Very elastic, cartoony animation with huge squash and stretch.
- **World:** Levels with an escape timer back through the same route.
- **Steal this:** A reacting face in the HUD gives the UI personality.

## Unpacking
`unpacking` · pictures from Steam · good for: world

- **UI:** No text UI; rooms tell the story.
- **Characters and shapes:** Objects are the characters.
- **World:** Cozy isometric rooms.
- **Steal this:** Tell stories with objects instead of words.

## Brotato
`brotato` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Shop between waves with clear stat cards.
- **Characters and shapes:** A potato with weapons: one silly idea, very readable.
- **World:** Single arena per run.
- **Steal this:** One funny main character idea goes a long way.

## ANIMAL WELL
`animal-well` · pictures from Steam · good for: world

- **UI:** Barely any UI.
- **Characters and shapes:** Small blob hero; huge animals as threats.
- **World:** Dark pixel world with glowing highlights.
- **Steal this:** Light on dark: glowing items guide the eye.

## Spiritfarer
`spiritfarer` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, world

- **UI:** Soft rounded UI; building menus as cards.
- **Characters and shapes:** Spirits are animals with simple flowing shapes.
- **World:** Your boat is your base that grows.
- **Steal this:** A base that travels with you (boat, train).

## Kingdom Two Crowns
`kingdom-two-crowns` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** No HUD: coins show as a pouch on your mount.
- **Characters and shapes:** Small pixel ruler on a horse.
- **World:** A side-scrolling kingdom with day and night.
- **Steal this:** Show money in the world rather than in a counter.

## Untitled Goose Game
`untitled-goose-game` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, characters, world

- **UI:** To-do list as a handwritten notebook.
- **Characters and shapes:** A goose with flat colours, no outlines.
- **World:** A small village in flat pastel colours.
- **Steal this:** A handwritten to-do list as the whole quest system.

## WEBFISHING
`webfishing` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, world

- **UI:** Chunky, cute UI.
- **Characters and shapes:** Animal characters with simple faces.
- **World:** A cozy social island.
- **Steal this:** A cozy hangout plus one simple activity is a whole game.

## Schedule I
`schedule-i` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Phone-style apps for every system.
- **Characters and shapes:** Simple low-poly people.
- **World:** A small town that expands as you grow.
- **Steal this:** A phone in the game is a natural home for many menus.

## PEAK
`peak` · pictures from Steam · good for: world, characters

- **UI:** Stamina bar that shrinks with injuries.
- **Characters and shapes:** Simple scouts; the fun is co-op physics.
- **World:** One tall mountain, procedurally different each day.
- **Steal this:** A single tall goal climbed together.

## R.E.P.O.
`repo` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters

- **UI:** Minimal HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Robot heads with big eyes; googly physics make them funny.
- **World:** Spooky mansions full of valuables.
- **Steal this:** Physics comedy plus fragile loot creates stories.

## Lethal Company
`lethal-company` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Retro terminal UI and a visor HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Simple suited crew; monsters with one rule each.
- **World:** Abandoned facilities, quota deadlines.
- **Steal this:** A quota and a deadline give a clear goal to a chaotic game.

## Among Us
`among-us` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, ui

- **UI:** Task list top-left; big full-screen meeting UI with votes.
- **Characters and shapes:** Crewmates: a bean with a visor and backpack; colour is the identity.
- **World:** A small spaceship map with rooms named by job.
- **Steal this:** A body shape so simple anyone can draw it; colour as identity.
