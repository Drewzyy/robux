# Teardown: Fortnite
Free-to-play battle royale that became a social space and a platform. Lessons: short readable matches, seasons, cosmetics that never touch power.

## Do this
- Make each round 15 to 25 minutes max with a visible shrinking goal and a clear end so a player can stop at a natural point.
- Run seasons of 8 to 12 weeks with a new theme, a new map change and a free track plus a premium track in the pass. A free track keeps zero-spend players in.
- Sell only cosmetics and convenience that give no combat advantage. Pay-to-win kills trust with kids and parents.
- Add a drop-in event once a season, at a fixed time, that changes the map for everyone. Announce it in-game 3 to 7 days ahead.
- Give constant small goals: daily and weekly quests of 3 to 5 each that finish in a normal session. See [[03-Game-Psychology/streaks-and-daily-rewards]].
- Make emotes and dances free to try in a lobby: self-expression drives sharing. Let friends squad with one tap. See [[03-Game-Psychology/play-with-friends]].
- Let skilled players show off: kill replays, a victory screen with a highlighted moment.

## Why it got huge
- Free, cross-platform, and playable on weak devices, so friends are on the same game.
- Cartoon look and no gore suited young players and streamers.
- Building added a skill ceiling that made clips go viral.
- Seasonal change meant the game looked new again without a new download.

## Why people play
- Win fantasy: only one squad wins, so the top-1 moment is rare and memorable.
- Hanging out: lobbies and squads are a place to talk while waiting.
- Expression: cosmetics, emotes, sprays.

## Why they stay
- Battle pass progress is a visible bar with a deadline. Sunk progress plus a date pulls players back.
- Mastery tracks: building, aim and movement all improve.
- The map changes, so knowledge needs refreshing.

## Why they come back
- New season launch day, a live event, a collab theme, a returning item shop rotation (items leave and return, which creates urgency).
- Friends invite friends.

## Numbers to start from (our suggestions)
- Battle pass: 30 to 50 tiers, one tier every 15 to 30 minutes of normal play; 80 percent reachable by a casual player in a season.
- Free track gets about a third of rewards.
- Daily quest set refresh at a fixed UTC hour, 3 quests, each under 10 minutes.

## Mistakes to avoid
- FOMO that is too aggressive: if a missed season cannot be recovered, kids feel pressure. Prefer rewards that return later.
- Timed offers that appear while a player is mid-fight.
- Copying shooters: gun combat needs aiming netcode that costs time. A small co-op or race round may be cheaper.

## For our Roblox games
Copy: rounds with an end, seasons with a free pass track, cosmetic-only selling, live events at a set time, easy squad-up, daily/weekly goals. Never copy: the name, map, skins, emotes, Battle Bus, logos, music or building UI art. Respect Roblox policy on paid random items. Related: [[03-Game-Psychology/reward-schedules]], [[03-Game-Psychology/lessons-from-classic-games]].

## Cheap zero-spend versions
- Seasons: a themed set of 20 cosmetics made from free or self-built parts, rotated by date.
- Live event: a server-wide countdown and a one-time map change using MessagingService; no paid tools needed.
- Squad up: Roblox friends join and party features are built in; add a "join friend" button.
- Pass: a simple table of tiers keyed by earned points, saved in the player profile.
