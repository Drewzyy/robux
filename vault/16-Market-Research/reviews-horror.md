# Reviews: horror games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Spend effort on atmosphere (lighting, sound, pacing) before monsters; players name mood as the top praise.
- Vary scares: after 3 playthroughs a repeated jump scare stops working, so randomize rooms and monster timing.
- Price and length must match: short games draw most complaints only when also pricey (Roblox is free, so keep sessions 10-20 minutes and add replay).
- Run a bug sweep for crashes and soft locks; bug complaints came second in the Steam study.
- Support co-op of 2-4 players; friends make fear fun.
- Give a tension rest every 2-3 minutes so scares land.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. Real dread and atmosphere. Phrasing: 'genuinely creepy, I felt oppressed.'
2. Randomness that keeps it scary. Phrasing: 'never the same twice.'
3. Co-op screaming with friends.
4. Classic homage feel. Phrasing: 'felt like old survival horror.'
5. Smart, fair monsters. Phrasing: 'I could learn its rules.'
6. Replayability. Phrasing: 'addictive, one more run.'

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Too short. In the Steam study 406 reviewers across 38 of 47 games said so (79 percent of those inside positive reviews).
2. Bugs and crashes (about 203 reviewers across 26 games).
3. Stops being scary after the first run.
4. Unclear objectives and cheap deaths.
5. Poor performance on weak devices.
6. Story or choices that feel shallow vs. the promise.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- Light and sound pass per room.
- Monster rules players can learn.
- Randomized room order and event timers.
- Checkpoint after every major scare.
- Voice or chat spots for co-op.

### Never do this
- Never rely on one repeated jump scare.
- Never make deaths unexplained.
- Never use real gore or content unsuitable for the age rating.
- Never copy a known monster's design.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] Scare variety list of at least 8.
2. [ ] Monster rules documented.
3. [ ] Randomization tested across 5 runs.
4. [ ] Crash and soft-lock sweep.
5. [ ] Low-end device test.
6. [ ] Co-op test with 4.
7. [ ] Rest beats between scares.
8. [ ] Clear objective in first minute.
9. [ ] Content rating questionnaire done.
10. [ ] Replay hook (unlocks or modes).

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: give players the loved things above for free and early, and remove the hated things before launch, especially unfair pay walls, hidden odds and bugs in the first 10 minutes.
- Never copy names, characters, art, music, logos or UI skins from any game named here. Keep prompts to buy player-initiated and show odds and prices plainly, since our audience includes children.
