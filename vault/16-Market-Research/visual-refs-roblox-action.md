# Visual references: Roblox action, horror, obby and party

30 games. Pictures are in `attachments/game-refs/<id>/` once downloaded (BrewixGames: Make the Game, Knowledge, Game References, Download Pictures). Open the pictures with Read to look at them; they are other companies' art, for study only, never to copy or upload.

## Do this
- Pick 2 or 3 games below that match the game you are making, open their pictures, and name the exact thing you are borrowing (a button style, a silhouette rule, a map layout).
- Borrow rules, not art: shape, colour logic, layout and feedback. Never trace or reuse anyone's art, names or characters.
- Read the 'Steal this' line of each picked game first; it is the one rule worth copying.
- For shape language basics see [[16-Market-Research/visual-refs-shape-language]].

## Blox Fruits
`blox-fruits` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Compact skill bar (Z X C V F) bottom-centre, level and bounty top-left, quest panel left; dense but always in the same place.
- **Characters and shapes:** Avatar plus big effect silhouettes: each fruit has a signature colour and shape of effects.
- **World:** Three seas of islands, each themed and levelled; you sail between them.
- **Steal this:** Each power gets one signature colour so players know what hit them.

## The Strongest Battlegrounds
`the-strongest-battlegrounds` · pictures from Roblox · good for: characters, ui, effects

- **UI:** Four ability slots with icons on the bottom; the rest is screen space for the fight.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars; character identity comes from moves, camera shakes and impact frames.
- **World:** A compact arena city that breaks under hits.
- **Steal this:** Impact frames, camera shake and slow-down on big hits make simple moves feel huge.

## DOORS
`doors` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, characters, ui, thumbnail

- **UI:** Almost no HUD; the room number on each door is the progress bar. Items appear in a tiny hotbar.
- **Characters and shapes:** Monsters each have one rule and one strong silhouette or sound cue (Rush, Seek, Figure).
- **World:** Procedurally chained hotel rooms with clear readable layouts; light and darkness do the pacing.
- **Steal this:** Use the room number as the progress bar, and give every enemy a single rule players can learn.

## Murder Mystery 2
`murder-mystery-2` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Round role reveal fills the screen ('You are the Murderer'); otherwise almost no HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars; knives and guns are the cosmetics that sell.
- **World:** Small detailed maps (house, office, hotel) with hiding spots; several maps rotate.
- **Steal this:** A full-screen role reveal at round start makes the game make sense in one second.

## Tower of Hell
`tower-of-hell` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, ui

- **UI:** Only a timer and a stage height bar; the tower is the UI.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars.
- **World:** A vertical tower of random section pieces in bright solid colours; kill bricks are red neon, so danger reads instantly.
- **Steal this:** Danger has one colour across the whole game; never use that colour for anything safe.

## Jailbreak
`jailbreak` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, ui, thumbnail

- **UI:** Clear team colours (orange prisoners, blue police); robbery markers on a minimap.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars in team uniforms; vehicles are the characters.
- **World:** An open city with robbery buildings spaced out so driving between them is fun.
- **Steal this:** Space your goals apart so travel itself (cars, helicopters) is a feature.

## Arsenal
`arsenal` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Kill feed top-right, weapon level shown big when it changes; very readable shooter HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars with big silly skins.
- **World:** Small arena maps with three lanes.
- **Steal this:** Show progress changes huge for a moment, then shrink them back out of the way.

## Natural Disaster Survival
`natural-disaster-survival` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world

- **UI:** Only a banner that warns which disaster is coming.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars.
- **World:** One island with a few buildings; each round a disaster destroys them.
- **Steal this:** Announce the threat by name a few seconds before it hits; players love preparing.

## Piggy
`piggy` · pictures from Roblox · good for: characters, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Simple item hotbar; chapter titles as big text cards.
- **Characters and shapes:** Monster has a simple, exaggerated shape (pig head, bat) that kids can draw.
- **World:** Small mazes per chapter with doors needing items.
- **Steal this:** Horror for kids: one monster with a very simple, drawable shape.

## BedWars
`bedwars` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, world

- **UI:** Kit selection screen with portraits; item shop as a tight grid with prices.
- **Characters and shapes:** Kits with strong silhouettes and colours; teams show by colour.
- **World:** Floating islands, one per team, around a middle island with resources.
- **Steal this:** Team colours everywhere (beds, islands, nameplates) so you never need to read a name.

## Blade Ball
`blade-ball` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters, thumbnail

- **UI:** One parry button and a big cooldown ring; the targeted player glows red.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars, swords and explosion effects as cosmetics.
- **World:** A round arena; the ball is the only thing that matters.
- **Steal this:** Show who is targeted with a clear colour on the player, not with text.

## Phantom Forces
`phantom-forces` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui

- **UI:** Realistic shooter HUD: ammo bottom-right, minimap, hit markers.
- **Characters and shapes:** Tactical soldiers; loadouts as attachment trees.
- **World:** Mid-size realistic maps.
- **Steal this:** Reference for a 'serious' look on Roblox; most young players prefer simpler.

## Evade
`evade` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, characters

- **UI:** Minimal HUD, a downed-player indicator and a timer.
- **Characters and shapes:** Nextbot chasers are flat images on a moving body: scary and cheap to make.
- **World:** Large liminal maps with long sight lines for chases.
- **Steal this:** A flat image chaser is cheap, funny and scary; lots of variety for little work.

## Rainbow Friends
`rainbow-friends` · pictures from Roblox · good for: characters, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Night counter and task list, very simple.
- **Characters and shapes:** Each monster is one colour and one shape idea (Blue is tall and drooling, Green is blind and long-armed).
- **World:** An amusement park at night in a single building with zones.
- **Steal this:** One colour plus one body idea per monster makes them easy to remember and draw.

## Dead Rails
`dead-rails` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, ui

- **UI:** Distance counter is the main goal; inventory is simple.
- **Characters and shapes:** Zombies and outlaws in a western style.
- **World:** A train moving through a long procedural western; the train is your base.
- **Steal this:** A moving base (train, ship) gives every run a direction and a timer.

## 99 Nights in the Forest
`99-nights-in-the-forest` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, ui, thumbnail

- **UI:** Night counter big at the top; campfire fuel bar.
- **Characters and shapes:** A deer monster with a clear silhouette; friendly animal characters.
- **World:** Forest around a campfire; light radius equals safety.
- **Steal this:** Use a light radius as the safe zone; the edge of the light is the drama.

## Build A Boat For Treasure
`build-a-boat` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, ui

- **UI:** Building tools on the side; the river of stages has a stage counter.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars; the boats are the creations.
- **World:** A long river split into themed stages with obstacles, ending in treasure.
- **Steal this:** A long linear course with themed stages is an easy progress bar.

## Flee the Facility
`flee-the-facility` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Computer hack minigame with a clear progress ring; beast warnings are sound and red light.
- **Characters and shapes:** The Beast has a hammer and a red glow.
- **World:** Small facility maps with computers spread out.
- **Steal this:** Teamwork objectives spread across the map force players to split up.

## Epic Minigames
`epic-minigames` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Big round title cards announce each minigame and its one-line rule.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars.
- **World:** Dozens of small arenas, each one idea.
- **Steal this:** Announce every round with a title and one rule sentence.

## Speed Run 4
`speed-run-4` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world

- **UI:** Timer and level number only.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars.
- **World:** Bright themed levels in a straight line, each with one visual theme.
- **Steal this:** Each obby stage gets one theme and one colour palette.

## Shindo Life
`shindo-life` · pictures from Roblox · good for: characters, ui, effects

- **UI:** Lots of ability slots and a big spin menu.
- **Characters and shapes:** Anime look with huge effect silhouettes.
- **World:** Big open maps.
- **Steal this:** Reference for flashy effects; too dense for young players.

## Funky Friday
`funky-friday` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui

- **UI:** Arrow lanes with very clear colours per direction; score and combo big.
- **Characters and shapes:** Avatars dance while you play.
- **World:** Stage per pair of players.
- **Steal this:** Rhythm UI: one colour per lane, big hit feedback on every note.

## Hide and Seek Extreme
`hide-and-seek-extreme` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world

- **UI:** Seeker reveal banner and timer.
- **Characters and shapes:** Players become tiny in giant rooms.
- **World:** Giant-size household maps (kitchen, garage) so hiding spots are furniture.
- **Steal this:** Make players small in a giant room; every object becomes a place to play.

## Combat Warriors
`combat-warriors` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Health and stamina bars bottom-left; weapon hotbar.
- **Characters and shapes:** Avatars with medieval weapons; weapon silhouettes matter.
- **World:** Arena maps with traps.
- **Steal this:** Show stamina next to health so players learn timing.

## Slap Battles
`slap-battles` · pictures from Roblox · good for: characters, world, thumbnail

- **UI:** Glove choice wall in the lobby; minimal fight HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** Each glove is one colour and one ability; the glove shape is the character.
- **World:** A floating arena island you can be knocked off.
- **Steal this:** One silly verb (slap) with many variants is enough for a hit.

## Forsaken
`forsaken` · pictures from Roblox · good for: characters, ui

- **UI:** Character select with portraits and role labels; ability icons bottom.
- **Characters and shapes:** Killers are famous Roblox figures restyled darker; strong silhouettes.
- **World:** Small maps for asymmetric chase.
- **Steal this:** Asymmetric games need each role's silhouette readable at a distance.

## Pressure
`pressure` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, characters

- **UI:** Minimal: room numbers and a few prompts.
- **Characters and shapes:** Monsters with distinct sound cues and lights.
- **World:** Underwater facility rooms; lighting and sound carry the tension.
- **Steal this:** Give every threat a sound before it appears.

## RIVALS
`rivals` · pictures from Roblox · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Clean modern shooter UI: round score at the top, weapon icons, crisp fonts.
- **Characters and shapes:** Plain avatars with weapon skins; skins are the monetisation.
- **World:** Small symmetric duel arenas.
- **Steal this:** Symmetric small arenas for 1v1 keep matches short and fair.

## The Mimic
`the-mimic` · pictures from Roblox · good for: world, characters

- **UI:** Story text cards and chapter titles.
- **Characters and shapes:** Japanese-folklore inspired monsters with masks and long limbs.
- **World:** Narrow corridors, lanterns, traditional buildings.
- **Steal this:** A strong cultural theme (folklore) makes a horror game feel unique.

## Fruit Battlegrounds
`fruit-battlegrounds` · pictures from Roblox · good for: characters, ui, effects

- **UI:** Move list on the right with keys; spin for fruit screen.
- **Characters and shapes:** Avatars plus big fruit-power effects.
- **World:** Arena islands.
- **Steal this:** Signature effect per power; reuse one avatar base.
