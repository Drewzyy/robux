# Teardown: Steal An Egg

Players find eggs, hatch pets, earn money from them, upgrade a base and treadmills, and steal eggs from others. It is the same "steal" template as Steal a Brainrot, with pets.

## Do this
- Reuse a proven template and add one twist. Here the twist is eggs plus treadmills.
- Make the valuable thing physical and carriable. A player carrying a big egg back to base is a visible risk that others can see and steal.
- Reward finding rarity: a bigger egg in a quiet server. This created a server-hopping meta (filter to fewest players).
- Do not build around the server-hop exploit. Give a safer alternative, such as protected hatching zones, to avoid empty-server hopping.
- Keep progression readable: egg, pet, income, base upgrade, bigger egg. Four steps, each with a visible number.
- Watch the platform ranking. Roblox's discovery favours engagement, so retention (return rate) beats clicks.
- Prepare to sustain it: a new egg tier or mechanic weekly.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Pure cloning. Several other "Steal a ..." games appear at tiny CCU.
- Letting hopping into empty servers be the best strategy.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: carry-and-defend value, readable four-step loop, a template plus one twist.
- Never copy: the Steal An Egg name, egg and pet art, the base layout. Avoid "Steal a X" titles.
