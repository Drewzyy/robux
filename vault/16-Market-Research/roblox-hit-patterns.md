# Roblox hit patterns

Use this as a checklist when you design a game. Items are ranked by how many teardowns show them and how much they seem to matter. Always plan for decay.

## Do this
- Before you build, write a one-sentence pitch a child can repeat ("steal from friends", "survive 99 nights", "dress to a theme"). Pass if you cannot.
- Pass the clip test: can a 10-second video show the hook with no explanation? Every 2025 to 2026 breakout does.
- Give the first success within 30 seconds and a visible number rising within 10 seconds.
- Add a shared, visible stake between players (theft, rating, co-op defence, a hidden role) in the base design.
- Plan a content beat every 1 to 4 weeks from launch day, and a large drop every 2 to 3 months.
- Budget for decay: assume the 30-day peak falls to 1 to 10 percent within 6 to 12 months unless you keep shipping.
- Sell cosmetics, convenience and status, not the win. Read [[07-Monetization/paid-random-items-checklist]] for any random item.

## Ranked checklist (most common first)
1. **One-sentence, clip-able hook.** Seen in all the 2025-2026 breakouts we read. Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights, Escape Tsunami, Dead Rails and Animal Hospital all name the whole game in the title. Check: title plus 10-second clip explains it.
2. **Visible number that goes up.** Income ticks (Steal a Brainrot, Steal An Egg), distance (Dead Rails), door count (Doors), nights (99 Nights). Check: a counter on screen within 10 s and a reward every 30 s ([[16-Market-Research/teardown-plus1-speed]]).
3. **A social stake in the loop.** Theft, voting, hidden roles, shared fire, parry duels. Check: removing other players would break the loop.
4. **Rarity ladder you can name.** 4 to 12 tiers (Brainrot lists up to 12; MM2 tops at godly; Adopt Me ages and neon). Check: the top tier earns or signals about 10x the one below.
5. **Event cadence.** Grow a Garden had 9 update days in 30; Adopt Me runs themed events (30-day peak 1.5M against 430K-465K typical); Steal a Brainrot ran Admin Abuse events. Check: a dated calendar for the next 8 weeks.
6. **Clear long goal plus short sessions.** 99 nights, 99 doors, ranks, rebirths. Check: both a 5-minute win and a multi-week goal exist.
7. **Collection that visibly shows status.** MM2 knives, Adopt Me pets, DTI outfits, Jailbreak vehicles. Check: other players can see your best item in under 5 seconds.
8. **Free path stays whole.** Monetization is cosmetics, speed, revives, slots, VIP. Check: a free player can reach every goal, only slower.
9. **Co-op or party scale.** Doors and 99 Nights run in small groups; Blade Ball and MM2 in lobbies. Check: friends can join in 2 clicks.
10. **Trading, only with controls.** MM2 gates trading at level 10 with 4 items per side. Pet Simulator 99 and Adopt Me use it for status. Check: a delay, a confirm screen, a level gate and a report flow before you open it.
11. **Rebirth or prestige reset with a visible boost.** Steal a Brainrot first rebirth about $500K plus two items. Check: the boost is visible within 60 seconds of the reset.
12. **Cheap to render and low latency.** Dead Rails used all spare CPU at 1M CCU (Roblox newsroom). Check: a stable 30 FPS on a low-end phone and a server that scales.

## Spike and decay patterns
- Fast spike, fast fall (Dead Rails, Animal Hospital, 99 Nights): clip-driven; held by updates for 2 to 4 weeks each.
- Slow base, event spikes (Adopt Me, MM2): collection and social carry the baseline; events lift it 3x.
- Old-school mastery (Tower of Hell): no progression, retention by skill and rivalry.

## Common mistakes
- Cloning a title pattern ("Steal a X", "Escape X For Brainrots", "99 Nights ..."): the copies sit at a few thousand CCU.
- Launching trading, PvP and random crates in game 1.
- Treating a one-month spike as a business. Plan year two on day one.
- Fixing monetization before the first 60 seconds works.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy: the hook sentence, the visible number, a social stake, a nameable rarity ladder, a dated event calendar, a free-first monetization path, plan-for-decay budgeting.
- Never copy: titles, characters, meme IP, maps, audio, UI or any real brand or celebrity from these games. Original IP only, free assets only.
