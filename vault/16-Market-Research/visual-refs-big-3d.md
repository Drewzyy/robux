# Visual references: Big 3D, competitive and character games

37 games. Pictures are in `attachments/game-refs/<id>/` once downloaded (BrewixGames: Make the Game, Knowledge, Game References, Download Pictures). Open the pictures with Read to look at them; they are other companies' art, for study only, never to copy or upload.

## Do this
- Pick 2 or 3 games below that match the game you are making, open their pictures, and name the exact thing you are borrowing (a button style, a silhouette rule, a map layout).
- Borrow rules, not art: shape, colour logic, layout and feedback. Never trace or reuse anyone's art, names or characters.
- Read the 'Steal this' line of each picked game first; it is the one rule worth copying.
- For shape language basics see [[16-Market-Research/visual-refs-shape-language]].

## Overwatch 2
`overwatch-2` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, ui

- **UI:** Ability icons bottom-right with cooldown numbers; teammates' portraits top.
- **Characters and shapes:** Every hero is readable in silhouette alone (Blizzard tests in black fill); shapes match roles (tanks big and square, supports softer).
- **World:** Maps with clear routes and a payload.
- **Steal this:** Test your characters as black silhouettes; if you cannot tell them apart, redesign.

## Apex Legends
`apex-legends` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui

- **UI:** Ping system: one button marks enemies, items and places; plus a loot UI with colour per rarity.
- **Characters and shapes:** Legends with distinct shapes and colours.
- **World:** Large map with named landmarks.
- **Steal this:** A ping wheel lets players talk without voice; great for kids' games.

## Marvel Rivals
`marvel-rivals` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, ui, effects

- **UI:** Comic-style UI with bold panels.
- **Characters and shapes:** Comic-book cel shading and thick lines.
- **World:** Destructible maps.
- **Steal this:** Cel shading with thick outlines suits Roblox's simple shapes.

## Fall Guys
`fall-guys` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters, world, ui

- **UI:** Huge round titles and counters; qualified players shown as a shrinking number.
- **Characters and shapes:** Jellybean bodies, stubby limbs, costumes on top: maximum cute, maximum clumsy.
- **World:** Candy-coloured obstacle courses with spinning bars, big rubber shapes, no sharp edges.
- **Steal this:** Soft rounded obstacles in candy colours read as fun, never dangerous.

## Rocket League
`rocket-league` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui

- **UI:** Boost meter bottom-right; ball cam toggle; score top-centre.
- **Characters and shapes:** Cars as characters; decals and toppers sell.
- **World:** One arena with clear goals.
- **Steal this:** A single arena and one ball can be a forever game.

## Team Fortress 2
`team-fortress-2` · pictures from Steam · good for: characters

- **UI:** Classic class HUD.
- **Characters and shapes:** The textbook of silhouette-based class design: each class has a unique shape and weapon outline.
- **World:** Clear team colours (red, blue) on every surface.
- **Steal this:** Team colour on the world itself, not only the players.

## Portal 2
`portal-2` · pictures from Steam · good for: world

- **UI:** No HUD; the gun shows which portal is next by colour.
- **Characters and shapes:** Robots with one eye: emotion from one light.
- **World:** Test chambers that teach one idea at a time.
- **Steal this:** Teach one idea per room, then combine.

## Destiny 2
`destiny-2` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui

- **UI:** The director map and gear screens are beautiful, minimal and grid-based.
- **Characters and shapes:** Armour silhouettes by class.
- **World:** Skyboxes with huge planets for scale.
- **Steal this:** Big skybox objects make small maps feel epic.

## ELDEN RING
`elden-ring` · pictures from Steam · good for: ui, world
