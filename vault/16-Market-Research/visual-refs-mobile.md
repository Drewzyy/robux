# Visual references: Mobile hits (Supercell, Pokémon, casual)

38 games. Pictures are in `attachments/game-refs/<id>/` once downloaded (BrewixGames: Make the Game, Knowledge, Game References, Download Pictures). Open the pictures with Read to look at them; they are other companies' art, for study only, never to copy or upload.

## Do this
- Pick 2 or 3 games below that match the game you are making, open their pictures, and name the exact thing you are borrowing (a button style, a silhouette rule, a map layout).
- Borrow rules, not art: shape, colour logic, layout and feedback. Never trace or reuse anyone's art, names or characters.
- Read the 'Steal this' line of each picked game first; it is the one rule worth copying.
- For shape language basics see [[16-Market-Research/visual-refs-shape-language]].

## Brawl Stars
`brawl-stars` · pictures from App Store · good for: ui, characters, thumbnail

- **UI:** Huge chunky buttons with black outlines and bevelled colour; very touch friendly. Supercell's UI is the industry template.
- **Characters and shapes:** Brawlers have big heads, readable silhouettes and one prop each; seen from above they still read.
- **World:** Top-down maps with bushes, walls and clear lanes.
- **Steal this:** Copy Supercell button style: thick dark outline, bright fill, small top highlight.

## Clash Royale
`clash-royale` · pictures from App Store · good for: ui, characters

- **UI:** Elixir bar at the bottom, card hand of four; chest slots with timers.
- **Characters and shapes:** Units are tiny but each has one huge feature (giant's size, wizard's fire).
- **World:** One symmetric arena per tier with a theme.
- **Steal this:** Each arena tier gets a new look; progress you can see.

## Clash of Clans
`clash-of-clans` · pictures from App Store · good for: ui, world

- **UI:** Resource bars top-right with caps; builder count; very clear upgrade timers.
- **Characters and shapes:** Chunky, cartoony troops with readable roles.
- **World:** A grassy village seen from an isometric view; buildings grow taller with level.
- **Steal this:** Make upgraded buildings visibly bigger and shinier.

## Subway Surfers
`subway-surfers` · pictures from App Store · good for: ui, characters, world

- **UI:** Score and coins top; mission panel between runs.
- **Characters and shapes:** Graffiti-style kids with bold colours.
- **World:** Three lanes of trains in a bright city.

## Candy Crush Saga
