# Reviews: tower defense games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Name the game clearly and teach in 60 seconds: place, upgrade, start wave. Devs report players confused by vague tower titles.
- Make free units viable for the full campaign.
- Show summon odds and a pity counter.
- Keep waves under 10 minutes for a first clear.
- Add co-op with shared cash for 2-4 players.
- Offer speed-up (2x, 3x) free after the first clear.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. Strategic placement. Phrasing: 'finding the perfect setup feels great.'
2. Collecting and upgrading units.
3. Co-op clears with friends.
4. Speed-up buttons.
5. Clear difficulty tiers.
6. Fresh maps and bosses.

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Pay to win summons. Phrasing: 'you grind forever for wishes' (Anime Defenders update backlash).
2. Heavy grind between stages.
3. Unit imbalance: one meta unit.
4. Long unskippable waves.
5. Confusing tutorial.
6. Lag at high unit counts.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- Tutorial level with forced first placement.
- Pity counter on summons.
- Unit tier chart showing roles.
- Speed toggle saved.
- Unit count cap and VFX throttle.

### Never do this
- Never gate story clears behind paid units.
- Never hide summon odds.
- Never copy anime IP characters (Roblox rules and copyright).
- Never stretch a wave past 90 seconds of idle.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] First clear under 10 minutes.
2. [ ] Free units can clear campaign.
3. [ ] Odds and pity visible.
4. [ ] Speed toggle.
5. [ ] Co-op test.
6. [ ] Unit balance sheet.
7. [ ] Lag test at max units.
8. [ ] Tutorial under 60 seconds.
9. [ ] Title tells what the game is.
10. [ ] IP check on every character.
