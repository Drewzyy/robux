# Reviews: shooter games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Tune gunfeel first: recoil, muzzle flash, hit marker sound, 100 ms max latency perception.
- Server-authoritative hits and rate limits to fight exploiters.
- Use safe spawns that never place players in view of enemies.
- Sell cosmetic skins only; weapon stats equal for all.
- Team balance by skill, with auto-balance on leaves.
- Aim assist on touch.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. Satisfying gunfeel.
2. Fair, fast rounds.
3. Weapon variety.
4. Skins to show off.
5. Team play.
6. Clear maps.

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Cheaters.
2. Pay to win guns.
3. Bad spawns.
4. Lag and desync.
5. Unbalanced teams.
6. Hard controls on phone.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- Hit marker and audio.
- Server validation.
- Spawn safety.
- Auto team balance.
- Touch aim assist.

### Never do this
- Never sell guns with better stats.
- Never trust client damage.
- Never use real weapon brand names or logos.
- Never tolerate cheat reports with no response.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] Gunfeel playtest.
2. [ ] Validation.
3. [ ] Spawn test 50 rounds.
4. [ ] Balance.
5. [ ] Touch aim assist.
6. [ ] Skins cosmetic.
7. [ ] Report tool.
8. [ ] Map readability.
9. [ ] Ping test.
10. [ ] Match start under 30 seconds.

- Copy the principle: give players the loved things above for free and early, and remove the hated things before launch, especially unfair pay walls, hidden odds and bugs in the first 10 minutes.
