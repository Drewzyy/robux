# Reviews: mobile casual and match-3 games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Show no more than one forced ad per 3-5 levels, and none in the first 10 levels; best is rewarded ads only (AppFollow: players accept ads they control).
- Offer a one-time purchase to remove ads.
- Remove or soften lives: Royal Match praise shows unlimited retries are loved.
- Smooth difficulty: no spike over 2x the fail rate of the prior level; check the fail rate per level.
- Never show ad creative that does not match the game.
- Save progress before every rewarded ad to avoid lost lives on crash.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. Relaxing short sessions.
2. Satisfying cascades and effects.
3. Free retries.
4. Rewarded ads they choose.
5. Clear boosters.
6. Daily gifts.

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Ads after every level.
2. Fake close buttons.
3. Sudden difficulty spikes that push boosters.
4. Lives timers.
5. Crashes after ads.
6. Misleading ads.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- Ad frequency caps.
- Fail-rate dashboard per level.
- No-lives mode.
- Save checkpoint.
- Remove-ads option.

### Never do this
- Never use fake close buttons or misleading creative.
- Never target children with spending pressure.
- Never spike difficulty to drive spend.
- Never copy Candy Crush art or names.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] No ads in first 10 levels.
2. [ ] Ad cap.
3. [ ] Rewarded only option.
4. [ ] Per-level fail rate.
5. [ ] No spike.
6. [ ] Lives soft.
7. [ ] Crash test after ad.
8. [ ] Remove ads.
9. [ ] Honest ad creative.
10. [ ] Child-safe spend rules.

- Copy the principle: give players the loved things above for free and early, and remove the hated things before launch, especially unfair pay walls, hidden odds and bugs in the first 10 minutes.
