# Reviews: racing games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Spend most dev time on handling feel; test with keyboard, gamepad and touch.
- Races of 60-120 seconds with a 20 second lobby.
- Give free starter cars that can win every race tier.
- Use light catch-up, not heavy rubber banding that punishes leaders.
- Server-sync car positions at 20 Hz or better to cut rubber-band lag.
- Add a ghost or time trial for solo players.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. Handling that feels good.
2. Speed and drift feel.
3. Car customization.
4. Racing friends.
5. Many tracks.
6. Quick restarts.

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Cars locked behind payment.
2. Rubber banding that feels rigged.
3. Lag and teleporting cars.
4. Poor controls on mobile.
5. Repeated tracks.
6. Long lobbies.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- Handling tuned on 3 inputs.
- Catch-up cap.
- Free car parity.
- Ghost mode.
- Track variety list.

### Never do this
- Never sell a car that is strictly faster.
- Never use scripted rubber banding that hides skill.
- Never use real car brand logos or models.
- Never leave players in lobbies over 30 seconds.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] Input tests done.
2. [ ] Lobby under 30 seconds.
3. [ ] Free car can win.
4. [ ] Catch-up cap.
5. [ ] Sync tested at 150 ms.
6. [ ] Track variety 5+.
7. [ ] Ghost mode.
8. [ ] Mobile layout.
9. [ ] Brand check.
10. [ ] Reset-on-flip button.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: give players the loved things above for free and early, and remove the hated things before launch, especially unfair pay walls, hidden odds and bugs in the first 10 minutes.
- Never copy names, characters, art, music, logos or UI skins from any game named here. Keep prompts to buy player-initiated and show odds and prices plainly, since our audience includes children.
