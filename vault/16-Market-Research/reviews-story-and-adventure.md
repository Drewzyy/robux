# Reviews: story and adventure games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Open with a hook in 60 seconds: a mystery or a goal, not a lore dump.
- Keep cutscenes skippable and under 30 seconds.
- Make choices visibly matter at least once per chapter.
- Chapters of 10-15 minutes, with a save point at each.
- Add one replay reason (alternate ending, hidden items).
- Mark quest goals on screen.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. Characters they care about.
2. A world worth exploring. Phrasing: 'every corner had a secret.'
3. Meaningful choices.
4. Good pacing.
5. Emotional endings.
6. Co-op story runs.

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Meaningless choices (The Quarry reviews).
2. Too short for the promise.
3. Unskippable long cutscenes.
4. Unclear objectives.
5. Bugs that break quests.
6. Genre mismatch: expecting action, getting talk.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- Quest tracker.
- Skip button.
- Chapter save.
- Branch flag per choice.
- Bug test of every quest path.

### Never do this
- Never fake choices without a visible result.
- Never block progress on a bug without a fallback.
- Never copy an existing story's characters or plot.
- Never gate the ending behind purchase.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] Hook in 60 seconds.
2. [ ] Cutscenes skippable.
3. [ ] One visible choice per chapter.
4. [ ] Chapter saves.
5. [ ] Quest tracker.
6. [ ] All quest paths tested.
7. [ ] Length matches promise.
8. [ ] Replay reason.
9. [ ] Ending not paywalled.
10. [ ] IP check.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: give players the loved things above for free and early, and remove the hated things before launch, especially unfair pay walls, hidden odds and bugs in the first 10 minutes.
- Never copy names, characters, art, music, logos or UI skins from any game named here. Keep prompts to buy player-initiated and show odds and prices plainly, since our audience includes children.
