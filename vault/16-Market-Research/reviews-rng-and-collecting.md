# Reviews: RNG and collecting games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Show odds as percentages on the roll button; this is also required for fair play with children.
- Add a pity counter that guarantees a rare after a fixed count (for example 100 rolls).
- Make rolls earnable by play (at least half of all rolls) and avoid selling rolls at escalating prices.
- Give duplicates a use (merge, fuse, shards).
- Add a collection book with progress percent.
- Add secure trade with confirm screen and a 5 second lock.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. The thrill of a rare pull. Phrasing: 'I got the secret one!'
2. Completing a collection.
3. Showing rares to friends.
4. Fusing or upgrading duplicates.
5. Free rolls for playing.
6. Cute art and sounds.

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Hidden or bad odds; press described this as child gambling.
2. Pay to roll with no pity.
3. Scams in trades.
4. Duplicates with no use.
5. Limited-time rares that pressure spending (FOMO).
6. Rare items losing value after inflation.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- Odds display and pity.
- Duplicate fuse system.
- Trade confirm and lock.
- Collection book.
- Time-limited items return later.

### Never do this
- Never sell loot boxes with hidden odds.
- Never use countdown pressure on purchase popups.
- Never allow cash-out style gambling.
- Never copy Pokemon or other IP creatures.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] Odds displayed.
2. [ ] Pity implemented.
3. [ ] Half of rolls free.
4. [ ] Duplicate use.
5. [ ] Trade lock.
6. [ ] Collection book.
7. [ ] Limited items return.
8. [ ] Spending cap or warning for minors.
9. [ ] IP check.
10. [ ] Scam report button.
