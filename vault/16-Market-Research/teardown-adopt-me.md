# Teardown: Adopt Me!

Uplift Games' pet, house and roleplay game, one of the longest-running top-10 Roblox games. Its pull is collecting pets and trading them with friends.

## Do this
- Build a collection that has two or three upgrade states per item (the game uses age-ups and neon-style fusion). Players keep one item for weeks.
- Run a seasonal event every few weeks with its own limited pets or items. Expect a 3x spike over baseline (430K-465K typical versus a 1.5M 30-day peak during Sugarfest and the Pet Wear launch, per RoWatcher).
- Add a free daily login reward that stacks. It costs nothing and gives a reason to open the game each day.
- Make the first pet free in the first minute so every player owns something to care for.
- Give players tasks (feed, play, sleep) that are small, repeatable and rewarded in seconds, not minutes.
- Add trading only after you have moderation, a trade confirmation screen with a delay, and scam protection (see [[16-Market-Research/teardown-pet-simulator-99]]).
- Ship cosmetic and home-decor sales next to the free path, so free players never feel blocked from the loop.

## 1. Identity
- Developer: Uplift Games. Launched 2017 (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED in session).
- All-time peak about 1.9M concurrent (RoWatcher and Rotrends list about 1.9M; one page dates it 2026-04-04). Older reporting says 1.92M once rivalled CS:GO's Steam peak.
- 30-day peak 1,501,665 around Sugarfest; typical range 430K-465K (RoWatcher, 2026). One snapshot shows 149K at another time of day. UNVERIFIED which hour each is.
- Update cadence: a themed event or feature roughly monthly. A Lunar New Year event and a Pet Releaser overhaul were reported for early 2026. No official 2026 roadmap was published (RoWatcher).

## 2. Core loop
Do pet and home tasks, earn bucks and potions, age-up or fuse pets, decorate, trade. Verb every 5 s: tap a task or a pet. Reward every 30 s: a task finishes. Session goal: complete the pet's needs and age it. Weekly goal: event pet or item.

## 3. Progression
Currency (bucks), potions, pet ages, neon and mega fusion, house furniture, event currency. Scarcity comes from limited-time pets that cannot be hatched later. Offline progress is UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Monetization
Robux eggs, pets, vehicles, gamepasses and house items; prices UNVERIFIED. Egg odds make it paid random items, so read [[07-Monetization/paid-random-items-checklist]] first.

## 5. First 60 seconds (INFERRED)
Spawn in a town, choose or receive a starter egg, hatch it, get a task bubble. First success inside 30 s.

## 6. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: friends play it, kid-friendly, pets are cute, the thumbnail shows a pet.
- Stay: aging a pet takes real days of tasks, so the investment grows.
- Return: events with limited pets, trading a rare item you hold, new updates each month, friends visiting your house.

## 7. Social
Server roleplay, house visits, trading with confirmation, family play. A Rowatcher piece notes roleplay games (Adopt Me plus Brookhaven) together held 900K+ players at one 2026 point.

## 8. Packaging and performance
Bright pet-forward icon. Runs on phones; a large share of players are young and on mobile (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED).

## Numbers
- 1.9M all-time peak, 430K-465K typical, 1.5M 30-day event peak (all RoWatcher, 2026).

## Mistakes to avoid
- Selling random eggs to under-13 players without odds shown. Check the paid-random checklist.
- Launching trading with no value controls. Scammers arrive in the first week.

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: slow-aging collectibles, seasonal limited items, daily tasks, friend visits.
- Never copy: the Adopt Me pet designs, names, house assets, UI, music, or the "neon" naming. Make original creatures.
