# Teardown: Steal a Brainrot

A shared-lobby base game where you buy meme characters that earn cash, and other players try to steal them. It briefly held the record for the most concurrent players of any single Roblox game.

## Do this
- Give every player a base with a visible, ticking income number. Players should see cash rise without pressing anything.
- Put the purchase source in the middle of the shared map (a conveyor in this game), so every player walks past every other player and sees what they own.
- Make stealing possible but recoverable: a stolen item should cost the victim minutes, not hours, and the victim needs a way to fight back (lock, alarm, retake).
- Use a rarity ladder with a few tiers you can name in one breath (Common up to Mythic and beyond). Each tier should earn roughly an order of magnitude more per second than the one below.
- Add a first rebirth that costs a clear target (about $500K in-game cash plus two named items, per one guide) and gives a small permanent income boost. Reset hurts, so make the boost visible in the next 60 seconds.
- Run limited live events that change the shared conveyor (this game's "Admin Abuse" events). Players return because the pool of items changes for a short window.
- Keep paid items to convenience and cosmetics: gamepass tools and speed, plus a cash-pack shop. Do not sell items that directly take from other players.

## 1. Identity
- Platform peak: 25.4M concurrent on 2025-10-07 (PocketGamer.biz; other outlets say 25.2M). First recorded as past 15M in a late August 2025 weekend, then second game ever past 20M after Grow a Garden.
- A Fortnite Creative spinoff reportedly passed 1M peak, and one outlet reported about 24M players across Roblox and Fortnite in a single day (Tribune, Sep 2025). Treat as press figures.
- Creator: not confirmed in the snippets read. UNVERIFIED.
- Update cadence: named rebirth updates (Rebirth 16 arrived 2025-10-04 with the Los Lucky Block, per Sportskeeda). Exact cadence UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Core loop
Buy a character from the conveyor, place it in your base, collect its income, buy a rarer one, steal from others, defend your own, rebirth. Verb every 5 s is walk, grab, or place. Reward every 30 s is a cash tick or a purchase.

## 3. Progression
- Currency: one cash currency, large numbers.
- Rarity ladder: guides list Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic and further tiers (one source says 12 rarities; UNVERIFIED).
- Rebirth: sacrifices cash and items for a small income boost.
- Bases: guides disagree on count (six or eight). UNVERIFIED.
- Scarcity: event-only items, for example a Lucky Block that appears only during Admin Abuse events and costs 250M cash on the belt.

## 4. Monetization
- Gamepasses exist for admin-style commands (HD Admin pass at 1,999 Robux per one guide) and gear. Full list UNVERIFIED.
- Random-chance items fall under [[07-Monetization/paid-random-items-checklist]]; check it before copying any.
- First-purchase price and premium anchor: UNVERIFIED.

## 5. First 60 seconds (INFERRED from guides, not observed)
Spawn at a base, see the conveyor, buy the cheapest character, watch cash tick up. The first success is under 30 s. Stealing is introduced by seeing other players run past.

## 6. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: a viral meme, a streamer clip, and a one-sentence pitch (steal from friends).
- Stay: income grows while you play, and a rare item is always one purchase away.
- Return: your base earns while you are away (check this per game, UNVERIFIED), live events, and revenge on whoever stole from you.

## 7. Social
Shared servers, visible bases, player-versus-player theft. Server hopping is common. No trading in the core loop that we found.

## 8. Packaging and performance

## Numbers
- 25.4M peak CCU, 2025-10-07 (PocketGamer.biz).
- Trackers later showed the game far below the peak; the spike was a viral wave, not a floor. Exact late-2026 CCU UNVERIFIED.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Copying the chaos without the economy. The ladder and the rebirth cost are what keep players.
- Letting theft ruin new players. Protect players under about 10 minutes of play (our rule, not measured).

## For our Roblox games
- Copy the principle: shared-map purchase point, visible income, recoverable theft, rarity ladder, event-limited items.
- Never copy: the meme characters and their names, Italian-brainrot audio, the game title, thumbnail composition, admin-abuse branding. Use our own original characters.
