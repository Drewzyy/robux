# Teardown: Animal Hospital (Anomaly)

A 2026 breakout, published May 10, 2026 by Animal Anomaly. Coverage calls it a horror sensation. Its title and variants ("2nd FLOOR", an Anomaly tag, outfit and avatar spinoffs) suggest an inspect-and-notice anomaly format. UNVERIFIED.

## Do this
- Ship one big update at a time of rising momentum. Animal Hospital passed 580K on June 28, over 730K a week later, and reached 1.2M CCU after a major update with a new character and items (GoSuGamers).
- Make each update add a named character and items. They give creators something new to film.
- Keep the format simple enough for a clip: one idea, one scare, one reveal.
- Add a second floor or area as the "next chapter" to extend the story, as the "[2nd FLOOR]" name implies.
- Let the avatar outfits or items spill into other games' catalog (many "Outfits" and "Avatars" spinoffs exist). That shows demand for the look, so offer free avatar-style cosmetics inside your game instead.
- Plan the decay curve: 30-day change was down 50 to 64 percent on RoWatcher pages in autumn 2026.
- Do not split variants onto many IDs at the same time (event, x2, update variants all exist).

## 5. Why players start, stay, return
- Start: horror clips on short video, a free game with a hook.
- Stay: new characters and items; speculation.
- Return: each update. The graph shows it works for about 2 to 4 weeks at a time.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Copying a horror skin before checking the loop. We could not read the loop, so research it in the live game before using the idea.
- Building a game whose life depends on one large update every few weeks.

## Open questions for our own research
- Read the live game's loop, shop and first minute before using any idea from it.
- Check whether update days line up with the CCU jumps (GoSuGamers ties the 1.2M peak to one update).
- Record D1 and D7 only from our own dashboard; no public source has them.
