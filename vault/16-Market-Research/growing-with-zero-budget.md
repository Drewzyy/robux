# Growing with zero budget
Organic tactics developers say worked without buying ads. Paid ads are out of scope for us, and several threads say small ad budgets (a few thousand Robux) are wasted anyway.

## Do this
- Make the game worth sharing first; every free channel below fails if players leave in the first minute.
- Post short gameplay clips (15 to 45 seconds) of one funny or impressive moment per update. Posters report free followers from YouTube Shorts and TikTok; one thread mentions about half a million visits from a single TikTok-driven game (UNVERIFIED).
- Run a Roblox group and a Discord (where the age rules allow) for update news, bug reports and ideas. Post update notes in both every release.
- Hand out free codes to small creators (1 to 10k subscribers) so they can give their audience a reward. One case study used an exclusive free rare pet code (illustrative, UNVERIFIED).
- Keep a public update log and post it each release; players and returning lapsed players see something new.
- Ask for honest DevForum and community feedback, but expect few steady players from it unless you are known.

## Channel notes
| Channel | What people report | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Short video (TikTok, YT Shorts) | Free reach; recommendation matches viewers to interests | Inconsistent; spikes fade; keep clips honest to the real game |
| Small YouTubers | Easier to get a play than big names | Need a code or private link; no pay-to-play |
| Discord | Bug reports, feedback, hype | Many servers ban promo; follow rules; age limits apply |
| Reddit (r/roblox, r/robloxgamedev) | Some free players | One case study calls r/roblox unforgiving; read self-promo rules first |
| DevForum | Feedback and peer eyes | Rarely steady players |
| Roblox group | Membership hub, perk delivery | Perks must be fair and optional |
| In-game codes | Gives creators and updates a hook | Do not make codes feel like a requirement |

## Group and code rules (child safe)
- A permanent small group bonus can drive joins (a simulator reported 15k to 220k members with a 10% coin bonus, UNVERIFIED). Keep it small and never block play behind it.
- Codes are free, shown clearly, with an end date if timed. No fake scarcity countdowns.
- Never ask a player to invite friends in exchange for a random reward; a fixed, clearly stated reward is fine.
- Do not direct young players to private chats or off-platform places in a way Roblox rules forbid; check the current Roblox community standards.
- Credit creators and thank players publicly without naming minors' personal details.

## Tactics ranked by effort versus reported payoff (our judgment)
1. Weekly update clip and update log (low effort, steady).
2. Codes for small creators (low cost, moderate).
3. Group and Discord upkeep (moderate effort, builds a core).
4. Reddit and DevForum posts (low reach, feedback value).
5. Chasing viral trends (high risk; see below).

## What goes wrong
- A TikTok spike without a return reason: one blog argues it harms retention. Plan something for day two.
- Posting the same link to many servers gets you banned or muted.
- Small creators who get a bad first minute will not return.
- Copying a competitor's hype clip risks trust and IP problems.

## Launch week checklist (no money)
- Day -7: group, Discord, update log page ready, first clip scripted.
- Day -3: code list ready for five small creators.
- Day 0: publish, post one clip, monitor the first-join exit rate.
- Day 1 to 7: fix the worst drop-off, post one small update, answer every bug report.

## For our Roblox games
Copy: the weekly clip, update log, small-creator codes, fair group perk and community feedback habit. Never copy: another game's name, logo, hype video, music or voice-over; spam posting; or any paid sponsorship, ad or boost. Keep all rewards optional, odds shown, and purchase prompts only after a player tap.
