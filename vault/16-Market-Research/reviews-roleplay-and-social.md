# Reviews: roleplay and social games
What players say makes them stay and what makes them quit in this niche, written as rules for building. Evidence is thin and mostly secondary, so treat rankings as hypotheses to test with our own data.

## Do this
- Fill the world with many small things to do (jobs, props, vehicles) so players self-direct.
- Add easy ways to meet: emote wheel, friend-invite button, group homes.
- Ship moderation tools on day one: mute, report, block, kick, private servers.
- Keep paid items cosmetic or convenience (extra houses, skins).
- Keep chat filtered and on by default for ages under 13.
- Update monthly with a small new location or item.

## Top 6 loved things (paraphrased review language)
1. Freedom to act out stories. Phrasing: 'I can be anything I want.'
2. Customizing houses and outfits.
3. Meeting friends. Phrasing: 'it feels like hanging out at a mall.'
4. Lots of vehicles and props.
5. Events that bring the server together.
6. Low pressure with no forced goals.

## Top 6 hated things (paraphrased review language)
1. Trolls, bullies and creepy behavior (a recurring community complaint, anecdotal).
2. Admin abuse and unfair bans.
3. Paywalled houses, cars and gamepasses (Meep City and Bloxburg criticism, see sources).
4. Lag in full servers.
5. Unfair in-game pay or reward changes (Bloxburg pay protest).
6. Nothing new for months.

## Review-to-mechanic translation

### Build this
- Report and mute buttons in the main HUD.
- Server size cap and private server option.
- Prop and job variety list.
- Cosmetic-only paid tier.
- Update calendar.

### Never do this
- Never allow unmoderated private chat spaces for children.
- Never change in-game pay or prices without notice.
- Never let admins ban without a log.
- Never copy named places or brand logos.

## 10-point pre-launch checklist
1. [ ] Report, mute and block work.
2. [ ] Safe chat defaults on.
3. [ ] Private servers work.
4. [ ] 20 things to do in first area.
5. [ ] Lag test at full server.
6. [ ] Paid items cosmetic.
7. [ ] Admin action log.
8. [ ] Event schedule for first month.
9. [ ] Friend invite button.
10. [ ] Update backlog for 3 months.
